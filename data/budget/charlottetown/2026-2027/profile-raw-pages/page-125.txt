  Simmons Artificial Turf Field
  Department: Parks and Recreation
      Project: Simmons Artificial Turf Field

  Project Description
  Complete the design, drawings and cost estimate for a new artificial turf field that can
  be used annually from April to November, accomodating up to 2,500 hours of use per
  season. Construction is expected to begin in May 2026, with completion by the end of
  July 2026.
