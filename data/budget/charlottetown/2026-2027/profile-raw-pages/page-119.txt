      Fire Engine Replacement
      Department: Charlottetown Fire Department
          Project: Fire Engine Replacement

      Project Description
      Replacement of three fire engines over the next five years, starting with Engine 4 at
      Station 2. These engines have exceeded their life expectancy as first response vehicles.
      In accordance with fire service code and standards, front line vehicles are to be
      replaced after 20 years of service. Replacing outdated vehicles will enhance firefighter
      safety and operational capabilities required in our growing City.
