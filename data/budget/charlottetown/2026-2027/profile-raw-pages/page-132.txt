Community Safety
Department: Charlottetown Police Services
    Project: Community Safety
Project Description
The department relies on field equipment to address its proactive and reactive policing
priorities. These funds will help complete the final fit out of equipment for the Incident
Command Post, address and support crisis/major incidents and events, and provide for
the continued investment in speed enforcement equipment (i.e., radar and lidar units) to
support our traffic enforcement capabilities and improve public safety.
