     Charlottetown Fire Department and
     Emergency Services
     Department Summary
     The Charlottetown Fire Department is a proud and robust combination fire department.
     It is supported by a strong and vibrant volunteer firefighter core alongside dedicated
     career members. The department is committed to protecting lives and minimizing
     property and environmental damage through safe and effective fire protection,
     prevention, investigation, rescue, and hazardous materials response capabilities.

     Operating under several City bylaws pursuant to the Provincial Municipal Government
     and Fire Prevention Acts, the department provides a range of programs and services
     including emergency response, training, public education, fire investigation, and fire
     inspection.

     Programs and Services Provided

        Public education sessions

        Fire inspection
