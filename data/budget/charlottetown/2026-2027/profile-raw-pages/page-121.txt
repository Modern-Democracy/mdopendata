      New City Website
      Department: Information Technology (IT)
          Project: New City Website

      Project Description
      The City’s charlottetown.ca website is an important platform for communicating with
      residents and delivering public services. A total of $255,000 has been approved in the
      2026/27 budget to support the development of a new municipal website. This
      investment will enhance accessibility, strengthen transparency, and improve digital
      service delivery for residents, businesses, and visitors. The redesigned website will offer
      a more user-friendly experience, expanded functionality, and improved access to
      important information and online services.
