    Introduction from the Chair of the Finance,
    Audit, Tendering and Administration
    Committee
                             The 2026/2027 Budget reflects a responsible financial plan
                             focused on protecting core services while making strategic
                             investments that support our growing community. This year’s
                             approach emphasizes prudent spending, long-term
                             sustainability, and delivering value for residents.

                             Throughout the budget process, Council remained committed
                             to transparency and good governance. Public engagement
                             opportunities, including neighbourhood town halls and online
                             surveys, were offered to ensure community input helped shape
                             priorities. Budget deliberations were conducted in open
                             session, livestreamed, and remain accessible to the public.

    In a time of continued economic uncertainty and rising costs, this budget maintains a
    strong focus on managing expenditures carefully while safeguarding the essential
    services residents rely on every day. Population growth continues to increase demand on
    infrastructure, public safety, and community amenities. Our responsibility is to respond
    thoughtfully—investing where needed while maintaining financial stability.

    The 2026/2027 Budget prioritizes:

       Core Municipal Services
       Public Transit
       Fire Services
       Sport and Recreational Amenities
       Technology Modernization

    This balanced approach addresses both operational pressures and long-term capital
    needs, positioning Charlottetown to manage growth responsibly while preserving the
    high quality of life our residents expect.


                                                                 Councillor John McAleer
