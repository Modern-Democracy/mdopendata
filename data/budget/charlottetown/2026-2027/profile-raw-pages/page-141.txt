      Sherwood Recreation Hall Upgrades
      Department: Public Works
          Project: Sherwood Recreation Hall Upgrades
      Project Description
      The Sherwood Recreation Hall is slated to be replaced with a new community center
      facility. A Masterplan for the area was undertaken as part of the City’s 2025-26 capital
      budget. Funding will be allocated to the detailed design of the new facility, based on
      the recommendations outlined by staff and external consultants.
