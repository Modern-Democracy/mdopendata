Active Transportation (AT)
Infrastructure
Department: Environment and Sustainability
    Project: AT Infrastructure

Project Description
Results to be achieved include:

 1. Design and build of Local Street Bikeways in Parkdale and Sherwood (approx. 3km)
 2. Design and engagement phases for Maple/Belvedere Ave
 3. AT wayfinding project continuation
 4. Design and engagement phase for Fitzroy St. or for AT projects in parkland
   properties that will become key AT connections (e.g. Rosemount Park, East Royalty's
   Montgomery Path, and similar)
 5. Build essential, minor AT connections (e.g. trail-to-road transitions).
