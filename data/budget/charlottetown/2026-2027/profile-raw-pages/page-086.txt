Public Works
Department Summary
The Public Works Department oversees the development, repairs and overall
maintenance of the City’s streets, sidewalks, and municipal buildings. The department
also maintains green areas, coordinates special events, maintains public parking and
assists in traffic control, among other duties.

Programs and Services Provided
   Maintains and repairs 260 km of streets
   Maintains and repairs 175 km of sidewalks and paved paths
   Capital construction and major project management
   Maintains public street parking and parking structures
   Urban beautification initiatives (flower beds, planters, public greenspaces, street
   furniture, Christmas decorations)
   Maintains 15 municipal buildings
   Traffic management (traffic signals and street lighting, traffic calming, signage and
   pavement marking)
   Manages stormwater
   Corporate fleet procurement and management
