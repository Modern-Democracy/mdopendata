Our Guiding Principles
These guiding principles unify our work and underscore our values:

Safety in all we do
Collaborating to make us stronger
Accountable to residents, ourselves and to each other
Welcoming as we grow
People-focused always

Our Progress and Next Steps
As we enter the final year of the 2022-2026 Strategic Plan, we’re proud to share that more than
90% of the actions are now complete or on track for completion by the end of 2026.

From climate action and transportation improvements to new recreation spaces and major
infrastructure projects, these milestones reflect our shared commitment to collaboratively
building a community for everyone.

To review the Strategic Plan progress report and keep up to date on plans for the 2027-2031
Strategic Plan, visit CharlottetownHall.ca/strategic-plan.
