  Victoria Park EV Charger
  Department: Environment and Sustainability
      Project: Victoria Park EV Charger
  Project Description
  A publicly available level 2 electric vehicle (EV) charger to be installed at a proposed
  location near the batting cages. This will expand the City’s network of EV chargers
  available to drivers of electric and hybrid vehicles.
