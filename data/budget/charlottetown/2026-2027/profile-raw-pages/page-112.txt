Downtown Tree Planters
Department: Enivronment and Sustainability
    Project: Downtown Tree Planters
Project Description
Raised concrete tree planters will increase tree canopy in the city's heavily urbanized
areas that have experienced significant tree loss over the years. Installation of one to
two tree planters/year, designed to provide needed protection for trees from plows,
cars and other common damages, while providing adequate soil and protection for
roots.

Strategic Alignment
    Community-Building and Placemaking
    Infrastructure and the Environment
