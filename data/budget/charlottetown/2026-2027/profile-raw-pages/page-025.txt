 City Government
 Office of the Chief Administrative Officer
 Summary
 At the core of Charlottetown’s governance, the Office of the Chief Administrative
 Officer plays a crucial role in leadership and coordination. It provides leadership in the
 growth and planning of Charlottetown by coordinating strategic initiatives, facilitating
 effective governance and ensuring the seamless execution of City policies, bylaws and
 resolutions that drive continued development and growth throughout the municipality.
 Administration supports the City’s Chief Administrative Officer (CAO) and the Mayor and
 Council through dedicated Executive Resources and a Procedures and Records
 Coordinator.

 The Office also provides City Council with advice on civic matters, and oversees the
 strategic management of City staff and their respective departments. The office
 supports Council on strategic initiatives with community input.

 Programs and Services Provided
     Ensures City resolutions and policies are in compliance
     Informs Council on the operation and affairs of the City
     Responsible for hiring, directing and managing all City staff
     Acts as a key liaison with all strategic partners


 Budget Highlights

     high level of service delivery to our citizens
