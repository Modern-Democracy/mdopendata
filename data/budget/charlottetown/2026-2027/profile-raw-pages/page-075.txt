 Planning and Heritage
 Department Summary
 The Planning and Heritage Department comprises two key sections: Planning Policy and
 Heritage and Development Planning. It manages the city's growth through community
 planning, sustainable development strategies, and land use regulation. Core
 responsibilities include issuing building permits, overseeing development agreements,
 and maintaining critical policy documents such as the Official Plan, Zoning and
 Development Bylaw, and Heritage Preservation Bylaw. Additionally, the department
 enforces zoning regulations, conducts inspections, and works to preserve and enhance
 the city's heritage assets, ensuring a balance between modern development and
 historical integrity.

 Beyond its regulatory role, the department's staff actively engages with residents,
 builders, and developers to assist with zoning inquiries and design reviews. The
 department also administers incentive programs that promote heritage preservation
 and collaborates with stakeholders to advance urban development initiatives that
 align with the City's broader strategic goals. In addition, the department facilitates
 incentive programs that support the provision of affordable housing, in partnership with
 the Province, as part of the City’s broader commitment to delivering diverse and
 attainable housing options for residents.
