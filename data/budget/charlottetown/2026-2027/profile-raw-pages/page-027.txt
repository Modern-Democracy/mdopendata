     Information Technology
     Department Summary

     Information Technology (IT) is responsible for managing the City’s technology
     infrastructure and supporting government operations. This includes providing technical
     assistance to staff, as well as maintaining the security of technology infrastructure and
     data. IT supports citizen engagement by providing digital services and ensuring data
     management and compliance with regulations.

     Programs and Services Provided

        Assesses potential security threats
        Troubleshoots issues and hardware repair
        Installs software and external devices
        Ensures compliance with standards
        Maintains infrastructure, licensing and vendor relationships

     Budget Highlights
        Continued implementation of Cityworks software to improve asset management,
        service request processing, and management of building permits and applications
        (including e-permitting)
        Development and implementation of a new City website to replace the existing
