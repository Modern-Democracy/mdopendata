      Specialized Equipment
      Department: Charlottetown Police Services
          Project: Specialized Equipment
      Project Description
      Police officer equipment ranges from the everyday essentials to specialized tools, each
      improving police outcomes. These tools carry the weight of justice, life and death.
      Officers must be outfitted to provide first aid, collect evidence, interview persons of
      interest, secure an area, subdue an assailant and more. Specialized equipment
      includes tactical equipment, firearms, less lethal weapons such as CEWs, helmets and
      goggles.
