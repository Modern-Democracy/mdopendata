North River Road & Capital Drive
Intersection Upgrades
Department: Public Works
    Project: North River Road & Capital Drive
             Intersection Upgrades

Project Description
The intersection at North River Road and Capital Drive is one of the busiest
intersections in Charlottetown, and is a primary gateway into the city from communities
to the north and west. This intersection has experienced increasing demand over the
past number of years, which, at times, results in significant traffic delays and
congestion.

This project is aimed at increasing the efficiency of the operations of this intersection,
particularly for northbound traffic on North River Road during the afternoon peak time.
As part of this project, the North River Road leg of the intersection will be reconfigured
to have two dedicated left turning lanes, and one shared through and right turn lane. In
addition, the sidewalk in the immediate area will be replaced, and the intersection will
have asphalt resurfacing undertaken.
