     Water and Sewer Utility
     Department Summary
     The Water and Sewer Utility is responsible for providing safe, clean water to residents
     and businesses in Charlottetown. It manages the overall water system, pumps and
     storage facilities that bring water from the watershed into the City system.

     The Utility also operates the Charlottetown Pollution Control Plant, also known as
     Wastewater Treatment Plant, which manages the collection and treatment system for
     all wastewater in Charlottetown.


     Programs and Services Provided
        Water Supply – 4 wellfields
        Water Distribution – 2 booster stations and 300 km of water mains
        Wastewater Collection – 40 liftstations and 260 km of sewer mains
        Wastewater Treatment – Charlottetown, Stratford and Eastern PEI
