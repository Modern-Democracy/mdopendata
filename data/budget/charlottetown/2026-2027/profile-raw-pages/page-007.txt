    City of Charlottetown Strategic Plan
    2022 to 2026
    In 2022, the City launched our Strategic Plan — A Community for Everyone — built with the voices
    of residents, stakeholders, Council, and staff. The plan establishes guiding principles that reflect
    shared values and foster a unified approach to the City’s work.

    Charlottetown City Council and its Administration will be:

        People-focused always
        Welcoming as we grow
        Collaborative to make us stronger
        Accountable to residents, ourselves, and each other
        Prioritizing safety in all we do

    The plan also identifies four key strategic pillars, accompanied by specific focus areas and
    commitments, to guide transformational change in the City.

    Our Vision
    Respecting the past. Embracing the future. Creating a community for everyone.

    Our 4 Pillars
