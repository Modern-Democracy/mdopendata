Environment and Sustainability
Department Summary
The Environment and Sustainability Department collaborates with internal staff to
manage the City’s natural assets, reduce carbon emissions, and ensure that City
operations are environmentally, socially, and economically sustainable. The department
partners with external groups and individuals to enhance Charlottetown’s natural
areas, ecosystem services, sustainable transportation, energy efficiency, food security,
and climate resilience. It oversees programs such as energy reduction initiatives,
climate action planning, management of natural areas and urban forests, water
conservation and flood prevention, food and waste programs, public transit and active
transportation.

Programs and Services Provided
   Energy reduction programs
   Climate action planning
   Natural areas and urban forest management
   Water conservation and flood prevention programs
   Food and waste programs
   Public transit and active transportation

Budget Highlights
   Full-sized transit buses, electric minibuses and chargers
   Neighbourhood bikeways and active transportation wayfinding signage
   Post-Fiona natural area restoration
   Home flood rebates
   Better Homes Neighbourhood Program
