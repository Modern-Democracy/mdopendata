  Exhibition Drive Reconstruction
  Department: Public Works
      Project: Exhibition Drive Reconstruction
  Project Description
  A full street reconstruction is being undertaken on Exhibition Drive from Kensington
  Road to Riverside Drive. This includes replacement of all underground infrastructure
  including water, sewer, and storm water systems. A new 3-metre-wide asphalt
  pathway is being constructed on the North side of the street.

  This project will provide significant benefit to the community through improved
  stormwater management, and improved capacity and reliability of utility infrastructure.
  Additionally, this project will provide an important, safe, active transportation
  connection from Kensington Road to Riverside Drive.
