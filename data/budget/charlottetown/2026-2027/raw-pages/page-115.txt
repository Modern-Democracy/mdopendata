  Public Transit Buses
  Department: Environment and Sustainability
      Project: Public Transit Buses
  Project Description
  New buses will be purchased to replace aging transit fleet. In 2026-2027: four full-sized
  buses to be delivered in the spring, six full-sized buses in December 2026 and two
  battery-powered electric minibuses delivered in March 2027.
