     Charlottetown Police Services
     Department Summary

     Charlottetown Police Services (CPS) is the municipal police force responsible for public
     safety, law enforcement, and crime prevention in the capital city. As the largest urban
     centre in the province, Charlottetown faces unique policing challenges due to its role as
     a government, tourism, and commercial hub. The city experiences significant population
     fluctuations due to seasonal tourism, post-secondary institutions, and large-scale
     events, placing added demands on police resources.

     CPS provides a range of services, including uniformed patrol, major crime investigations,
     street crime countermeasures, forensic identification, tactical response, traffic and road
     safety, and community outreach. The department also supports policing partners and
     other PEI communities through initiatives like the E-Watch public safety camera system
     and digital forensics.

     With increasing calls for service, rapid population growth, and evolving crime trends
     driven by technology, CPS is focusing on strategic growth to ensure public safety. With
     the introduction of provincial policing standards in December 2025, a key area of focus
     for CPS over the next two years will be to further review and adequately address the
     new standards at the local level.
