Communications
Department Summary
The Communications Department serves as the voice of the City, working in
collaboration with all departments to ensure that projects and initiatives are
communicated effectively so that residents, businesses and other stakeholders
understand the City's actions and their impact on the community. The Communications
Department also oversees community and media relations, and the City’s social media
channels, digital engagement platform, and website administration.

Programs and Services Provided
   Media relations
   Community relations
   Public affairs and issues management
   Internal communications
   Social media, digital engagement, and website administration
   Brand management
