Multi-Use Outdoor Facility
Department: Parks and Recreation
    Project: Multi-Use Outdoor Facility

Project Description
The outdoor sports facility located in the Elmer MacFadyen Memorial Recreational
Complex is at the end of its life; the city is unable to maintain the facility as an
aesthetically pleasing venue and to maintain safety standards.

A new outdoor facility will include a permanent artificial ice making system with a chiller
unit, new boards, lighting and playing surface. Ice-making has become challenging
due to changing weather conditions, and a permanent ice-making system will allow for
ice longer in the season, weather permitting. The City will engage with the public and
keep them up to date on the new and improved multi-use facility that will be functional
in all four seasons.
