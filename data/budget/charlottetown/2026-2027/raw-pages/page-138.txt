Pownal Street Parkade Upgrades
Department: Public Works
    Project: Pownal Street Parkade Upgrades
Project Description
The Pownal Street Parkade, a 465-vehicle parking garage located at 100 Pownal
Street, is currently undergoing concrete repairs and sprinkler system upgrades. This
facility was constructed in 1988, and while localized repairs have been carried out over
the years, the last major rehabilitation took place between 2008 and 2012. These
ongoing restoration projects aim to extend the service life of this facility.
