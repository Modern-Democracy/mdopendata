      Investigative Tools and Technology
      Department: Charlottetown Police Services
          Project: Investigative Tools and Technology
      Project Description
      The department relies on various tools and technology to support crime prevention and
      community safety efforts. As the community continues to grow, so to does the reliance
      on technology. This capital investment includes the continued expansion of eWatch
      and LPRs, BWCs, aerial support, surveillance equipment, digital forensic equipment and
      forensic equipment. It also supports a system to centralize and operationalize the Real
      Time Operations Centre.

      The Community Camera program (eWatch and LPRS) supports crime prevention and
      public safety efforts in real-time monitoring and investigation. These cameras provide a
      valuable tool for deterring criminal activity by increasing the likelihood of detection and
      prosecution. They also assist in gathering critical evidence, improving response times,
      and ensuring accountability in both police operations and public interactions. Public
      safety cameras enhance situational awareness, enabling officers to monitor high-traffic
      or high-risk areas, such as entertainment districts, transportation hubs, and public
      events, with greater precision.
