Vehicle Equipment
Department: Charlottetown Police Services
    Project: Vehicle Equipment
Project Description
From bumper to bumper, police vehicles are uplifted with dozens of pieces of
equipment to secure and safely transport people, specialized equipment, technology
and seized property. These funds will support outfitting the new vehicles coming online
and replacement parts for existing vehicles.
