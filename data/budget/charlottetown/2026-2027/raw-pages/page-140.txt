Traffic Calming
Department: Public Works
    Project: Traffic Calming
Project Description
In an effort to ensure the safety of all road users, new traffic calming devices such as
asphalt speed humps and cushions, curb extensions, signage, etc. will be deployed at
key locations throughout the city. This project aims to reduce motor vehicle travel
speeds, and as a result, may also promote the adoption of alternative modes of
transportation by improving user comfort.
