Human Resources
Department Summary
The Human Resources Department ensures that human capital aligns with the City’s
objectives and fosters a positive and inclusive workplace culture. The department
manages the employees lifecycle from recruitment to exit and ensures compliance with
regulations. The Human Resources Department is also responsible for administering
benefits, managing employee relations, labour relations, payroll and pension
administration.

Programs and Services Provided
   Ensures City complies with labour laws and regulations
   Manages payroll
   Recruitment
   Health and Safety
   Pension
   Labour relations
   Employee engagement
   Employee relations
   Wellness
