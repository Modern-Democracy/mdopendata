Transit Infrastructure
Department: Environment and Sustainability
    Project: Transit Infrastructure
Project Description
To improve safety and accessibility, annual bus stop improvements such as concrete
pads, shelters, benches, poles and signs will be added. Technology investments are
being made to improve fare payment and automatic passenger counting systems, to
help modernize and improve the transit experience and reduce ridership barriers, while
providing needed data for transit management and planning.
