Innovation Task Force
In September 2025, Charlottetown’s Chief Administrative Officer (CAO) Brad MacConnell
launched the Innovation Task Force – a cross-departmental working group tasked with
improving how technology, data, and business practices are aligned across City departments.
The task force brings together staff from across the organization to identify opportunities where
new tools, improved processes and data sharing can enhance the way the City operates and
serves residents.

A key focus of the task force is identifying practical ways to deliver services more efficiently, while
maintaining and improving service levels. By reviewing existing policies, reducing duplication, and
exploring the use of modern digital tools, the task force is working to find operational efficiencies
that reduce costs without compromising the high quality programs and services residents rely on.

The Innovation Task Force is an important part of the City’s ongoing commitment to modernizing
municipal operations. By proactively seeking efficiencies and smarter ways of working, the City is
positioning itself to manage costs responsibly and ensuring resources are used as effectively as
possible.
