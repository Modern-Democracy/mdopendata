East Royalty Landfill Old Road and
Active Transportation Pathway
Department: Parks and Recreation
    Project: East Royalty Landfill Old Road and
             Active Transportation Pathway
Project Description
This work will enable the public to access the Parkman Soccer Complex, Acadian Forest
& Trails, East Royalty Park and the neighbourhood of East Royalty from Acadian Drive.
The scope of work includes resurfacing a large section of the old asphalt road,
constructing a gravel driveway for the remaining portion and constructing an Active
Transportation Pathway along the full length of the road and driveway.
