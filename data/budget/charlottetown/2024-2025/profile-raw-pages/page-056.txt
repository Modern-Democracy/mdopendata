Victoria Park Shoreline Protection
Department: Parks and Recreation
    Project: Victoria Park Shoreline
             Protection
Project Description
This project changes around 245 meters of shoreline in
Victoria Park from sandstone to granite stones. The
current sandstone has lost volume and no longer protects
the area.

Strategic Alignment
   Community-Building and Placemaking
   Infrastructure and the Environment

Results to be Achieved
This project will make the park’s shoreline look better and will be more appealing for visitors to
the park. This will also protect infrastructure like boardwalk, roadway, swimming pool,
playground, and neighbouring properties.
