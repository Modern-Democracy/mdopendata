    Project: Wastewater Treatment

Project Description
This project includes upgrades to the Charlottetown
Wastewater Treatment Plant and collection system
liftstations.

Strategic Alignment
   Infrastructure and the Environment

Results to be Achieved
Upgrading the Wastewater Treatment Plant and collection system will ensure the City
continues to protect the Charlottetown Harbor water quality and the many benefits that
provides, as well as ensuring efficient plant operations.
