Hillsborough Park Community
Centre Upgrades
Department: Public Works
    Project: Hillsborough Park Community Centre
             Upgrades

Project Description
This project will upgrade the community centre on Hillsborough Park to make it more
accessible and usable. It will replace the wheelchair lift with an elevator, add a lobby and
change the front of the building.

Strategic Alignment
    Community-Building and Placemaking
    Infrastructure and the Environment
    Governance and Civil Engagement

Results to be Achieved
This project will make the community centre more inclusive, improve the exterior and create a
more enjoyable space for all.
