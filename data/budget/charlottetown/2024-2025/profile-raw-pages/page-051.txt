Self Contained Breathing
Apparatus Replacement
Department: Charlottetown Fire Department
    Project: Self Contained Breathing
             Apparatus Replacement

Project Description
This project will ensure the reasonable and incremental replacement and upgrade of these
vital firefighter life safety components. All members involved at structure fires, car fires,
wildland fires, hazardous materials incidents and all other dangerous environments will wear
these self contained breathing apparatus.

Strategic Alignment
   Community-Building and Placemaking

Results to be Achieved
Firefighters will experience vastly improved firefighting, search and rescue and rapid
intervention activities.
