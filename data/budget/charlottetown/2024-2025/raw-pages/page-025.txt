Finance
Department Summary
The Finance Department oversees all fiscal responsibilities in the City, including short-and long-term budget
monitoring. It also manages purchases from City departments and insurance coverage for all City properties. It
provides clear and complete information, along with support, to enable the best strategic decisions for the City.


Services Provided
   •   Short-and long-term operating and capital financial plans and budgeting
   •   Monitors budget
   •   Manages cash
   •   Billing and accounting for city revenues
   •   Accounts payable for all city departments
   •   Financial reporting and prepares annual audit documentation
