Charlottetown Fire Department
Department Summary
The Charlottetown Fire Department protects lives and limits damage to property and the environment through
the development and implementation of fire protection, fire prevention, fire investigation, rescue and hazardous
material response and mitigation practices and procedures in the City. The department is guided by several
City bylaws issued under the authority of the Provincial Municipal Governance and Fire Prevention Acts.


Programs and Services Provided
   •   Emergency response
   •   Training
   •   Public education sessions
   •   Fire investigation
   •   Fire inspection
