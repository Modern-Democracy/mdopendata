Wellfield Protection
Department: Water and Sewer Utility
    Project: Wellfield Protection

Project Description
This project proposes purchasing land within wellfield
protection zones.

Strategic Alignment
   Infrastructure and the Environment

Results to be Achieved
Expanding the City’s possession of land by wellfield protection zones will increase the
municipality’s ability to continue protecting its award winning groundwater quality and
enhance natural areas.
