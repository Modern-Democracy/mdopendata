Mayor and Council
Department Summary
Charlottetown City Council is the governing body for the City. It is comprised of a Mayor and ten councillors
representing ten wards within the city. The Mayor is elected at large, and councillors are elected by ward with
elections held every four years.


Services Provided
   •   Ensuring general welfare of the City
   •   Approving policies, programs and services
   •   Passing resolutions and bylaws
   •   Approving budget
