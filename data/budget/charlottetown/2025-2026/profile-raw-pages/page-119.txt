Artificial Turf Field
Department: Parks and Recreation
    Project: Artificial Turf Field

Project Description
Complete design and drawings and cost estimate for one artificial turf field that can be
used from April to November every year for up to 2,500 hours of use per season.

Construction is expected to start by September 2025.
