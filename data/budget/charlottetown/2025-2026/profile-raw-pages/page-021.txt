City Government
Office of the Chief Administrative Officer
Summary
At the core of Charlottetown’s governance, the Office of the Chief Administrative
Officer plays a crucial role in leadership and co-ordination. It provides leadership in the
growth and planning of Charlottetown by co-ordinating strategic initiatives, facilitating
effective governance and ensuring the seamless execution of policies, bylaws and
resolutions that drive continued development and growth throughout the City. The
office supports the City’s Chief Administrative Officer (CAO), the Mayor and City Council
through dedicated Executive Assistants and a Procedures and Records Co-ordinator.

The Office also provides City Council with advice on civic matters, oversees the
strategic management of City staff and their respective departments. The Office
supports Council on strategic initiatives with community input.

Programs and Services Provided
   Ensures City resolutions and policies are in compliance
   Informs Council on the operation and affairs of the City
   Responsible for hiring, directing and managing all City staff
   Acts as a key liaison with all strategic partners

Budget Highlights
   Focus on prudent fiscal management and making decisions based on continued
   high level of service delivery to our citizens
   Recognition of aging infrastructure and the need for strategic asset management
   Building on lasting partnerships to support our strategic plan
   Strategically addressing the growth of our city with the support of partner funding
   programs
