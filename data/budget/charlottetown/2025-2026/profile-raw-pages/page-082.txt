Public Works
Department Summary
The Public Works Department oversees the development, repairs and overall
maintenance of the City’s streets, sidewalks, and municipal buildings. The department
also maintains green areas, coordinates special events, maintains public parking and
assists in traffic control, among other duties.

Programs and Services Provided
   Capital construction
   Maintains public street parking
   Urban beautification
   Maintains 14 municipal buildings
   Traffic management (signals, lights, marking)
   Manages stormwater
   Maintains and repairs 260 km of streets
   Maintains and repairs 175 km of sidewalks and paved paths

Budget Highlights
   Heartz Hall Upgrades
   Planning for Sherwood Hall Upgrades
   Pownal Street and Queen Street Parkade Upgrades
   Public Works Large Fleet Replacement
   Parks and Recreation Small Fleet Replacement
   Replacement of Traffic Lights
   Replacement of Storm Water System on Exhibition Drive
