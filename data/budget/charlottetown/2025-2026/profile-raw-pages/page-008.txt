Our Guiding Principles
These guiding principles unify our work and underscore our values:

Safety in all we do
Collaborating to make us stronger
Accountable to residents, ourselves and to each other
Welcoming as we grow
People-focused always

Engagement
Public engagement is essential for responsive governance. Since gathering meaningful
feedback on the City’s financial plan within a short timeframe can be challenging, the City
remains committed to keeping residents informed about programs and services while listening to
their input year-round. In preparing the 2025-26 budget, the City used various opportunities to
engage with residents and gather feedback:

    The 2023 Community Survey, which provided thoughts and opinions on municipal programs
    and services from a statistically representative sample of Charlottetown’s residents
    Committee presentations where department managers presented their budget requests to
    the respective committees and participated in discussions
    A budget-specific project on CharlottetownHall.ca where residents were invited to
    participate in a pre-budget survey to indicate their priorities for City investments.
    In-person presentations from stakeholder groups and grant recipients, outlining their
    financial planning objectives.

These events have been promoted through social media, mainstream media, the City’s website
and CharlottetownHall.ca. All Budget Committee meetings have been held publicly, live-
streamed and are available for review on the City’s YouTube channel.
