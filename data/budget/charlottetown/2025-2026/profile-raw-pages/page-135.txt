 New Traffic Lights
 Department: Public Works
     Project: New Traffic Lights

Project Description
This work will involve the replacement of traffic signals with upgraded technology and
hardware. The focus for the 2025/26 fiscal year will be on the Grafton Street corridor
which includes Weymouth, Prince and Queen Streets.
