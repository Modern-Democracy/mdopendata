Communications
Department Summary
The Communications Department serves as the voice of the City, working in
collaboration with all City departments to ensure that projects and initiatives are
communicated effectively so residents, businesses and other stakeholders understand
the City's actions and their impact on the community. The Communications Department
also oversees community and media relations, and the City’s social media channels
and website administration.

Programs and Services Provided
   Media relations and training
   Internal communications
   Public affairs
   Community relations
   Social media creation and web content administration
   Brand management
   Issues management

Budget Highlights
   Status quo budget
   Three full-time employees
