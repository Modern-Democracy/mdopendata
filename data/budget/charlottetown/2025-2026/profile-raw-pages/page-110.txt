Bikeshare Program
Department: Environment and Sustainability
    Project: Bikeshare Program
Project Description
A bikeshare program in Charlottetown to allow residents and visitors to reduce their
reliance on cars and travel throughout the city by bicycle. A selection of electric and
traditional bikes will be offered. An RFP will be issued to select a third-party vendor to
deliver a two-year bikeshare pilot. Depending on bids received, this would include
between 60 and 180 bikes distributed throughout Charlottetown at up to 32 stations for
use by the public.

Strategic Alignment
    Community-Building and Placemaking
    Governance and Civic Engagement
    Infrastructure and the Environment
