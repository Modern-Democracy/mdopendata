Charlottetown Fire Department and
Emergency Services
Department Summary
The Charlottetown Fire Department is dedicated to protecting lives and minimizing
property and environmental damage through effective fire protection, prevention,
investigation, rescue and hazardous material response. The department operates
under several City bylaws issued under the Provincial Municipal Governance and Fire
Prevention Acts. Its programs and services include emergency response, training, public
education sessions, fire investigation and fire inspection.

Programs and Services Provided
   Emergency response
   Training
   Public education sessions
   Fire investigation
   Fire inspection
