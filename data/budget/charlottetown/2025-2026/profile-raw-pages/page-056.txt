Mayor and Council
Summary
Charlottetown City Council is comprised of a Mayor and ten councillors representing 10
wards within the city. The Mayor is elected at large and Councillors are elected by ward
with elections held every four years.

Programs and Services Provided
   Safeguarding the well-being of the City and its residents
   Ensuring policies, programs and services align with the provincial Municipal
   Government Act that sets the governance framework for all municipalities
   Adopting resolutions and enacting bylaws
   Overseeing the budget approval process, including establishing capital and
   operating budget priorities

Budget Highlights
   Initiatives that actively engage City residents, fostering a sense of belonging and
   encouraging civic participation. By supporting community programs and public
   engagement efforts, Council helps strengthen connections among residents and
   promotes good citizenship
