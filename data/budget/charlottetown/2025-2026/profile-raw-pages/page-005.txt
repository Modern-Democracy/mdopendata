Introduction from the Chair of the Finance,
Audit, Tendering and Administration
Committee
                       The 2025/2026 budget presents a solid financial plan for the City of
                       Charlottetown, one that touches all programs and services with a
                       focus on effectively managing growth through careful and planned
                       spending.

                       Throughout this year’s budget process, we have remained
                       committed to the principles of transparency and good governance.
                       In-person and online engagement opportunities were held to
                       ensure the budget reflects the needs of the community. Every effort
                       was taken to ensure meetings were held in open session,
                       livestreamed and can be viewed online by the public.

This budget reflects a prudent and strategic approach to spending in the wake of
unpredictable changes in the global economy and fiscal realities closer to home. In
Charlottetown, rapid population growth has created tremendous opportunities and also
increased demand on infrastructure and services. We are committed to managing
public finances responsibly and continue to invest in critical services including transit, fire,
sport and community infrastructure, and water and sewer.

As you look through this budget book, I’m confident the figures will show an appropriate
balance in addressing capital and operational needs that will help us prepare for growth
while maintaining the excellent quality of life our residents deserve.




                                                                Councillor John McAleer
