Aeration Tank Rehabilitation
Department: Water and Sewer Utility
    Project: Aeration Tank Rehabilitation
Project Description
Aeration Tank Rehabilitation at the Charlottetown Pollution Control Plant which includes
replacement and enhancement of wastewater aeration basin components at the
Charlottetown Pollution Control Plant, minimizing process downtime and increasing
resiliency.
