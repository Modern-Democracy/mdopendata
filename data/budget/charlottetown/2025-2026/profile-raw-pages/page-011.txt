1. Define Budget Process and Objectives: Budget process is presented to Council and
   objectives are defined.
2. Preliminary Budget Preparation: The budget is prepared taking into consideration Council’s
   objectives and strategic priorities.
3. Public Engagement/Surveys: The budget survey is made available to gather public opinion
   on the services that matter most to Charlottetown residents.
4. Public Meeting: Administration consults with the public to ensure residents have an
   opportunity to ask questions and provide feedback.
5. Internal Staff Review: The budget is reviewed by Senior Administration ensuring the proposed
   budget aligns with the public feedback and Council’s strategic priorities.
6. Budget Refinement - The budget is refined based on the feedback to Committee/Council.
7. City Council Review and Approval: The final City budget is reviewed by Council and
   approved prior to March 31.
8. Budget Monitoring/Forecasts: Administration monitors expenditures throughout the year to
   ensure adherence to the approved budget.
