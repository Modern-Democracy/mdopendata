Wellfield Protection
Department: Water and Sewer Utility
    Project: Wellfield Protection
Project Description
This project proposes purchasing land within wellfield protection zones. Expanding the
City’s possession of land by wellfield protection zones will increase the municipality’s
ability to continue protecting its award winning groundwater quality and enhance
natural areas.
