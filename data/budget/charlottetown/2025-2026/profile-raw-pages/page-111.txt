Public Transit Buses
Department: Environment and Sustainability
    Project: Public Transit Buses
Project Description
Diesel buses and electric minibuses to replace aging fleet and deliver on fleet
expansion to allow continued public transit service. Three diesel buses are planned for
delivery in December 2025. Bids for an additional four diesel buses closes in April 2025
for delivery in 2026. In April, planning will begin for four electric minibuses and charging
infrastructure.

Strategic Alignment
    Community-Building and Placemaking
    Governance and Civic Engagement
    Infrastructure and the Environment
