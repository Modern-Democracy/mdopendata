Finance
Department Summary
The Finance Department manages all fiscal responsibilities for the City. It oversees
procurement for City departments and handles insurance coverage for all City
properties. The department is committed to providing clear and comprehensive
information, as well as support, to facilitate informed strategic decisions for the City. It
is responsible for developing and managing short- and long-term financial plans,
capital and operating budgets and cash flow monitoring. The department is also
responsible for billing and accounting for city revenues, accounts payable for all
departments, preparation of the annual financial statements, and the annual year end
audit.

Programs and Services Provided
   Short-and long-term operating and capital financial planning and budgeting
   Budget monitoring
   Cash management
   Billing and accounting for city revenues
   Accounts payable for all city departments
   Financial reporting
   Procurement

Budget Highlights
   Status quo budget with some additional funding for depreciation and interest tied
   to the capital budget
