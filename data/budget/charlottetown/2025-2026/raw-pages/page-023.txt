Information Technology
Department Summary

Information Technology (IT) is responsible for managing the City of Charlottetown’s
technology infrastructure and supporting various government operations. This includes
providing technical assistance to staff, as well as maintaining the security of
technology infrastructure and data. IT supports citizen engagement by providing digital
services and ensuring data management and compliance with regulations.

Programs and Services Provided

   Assesses potential security threats
   Troubleshoots issues and hardware repair
   Installs software and external devices
   Ensures compliance with standards
   Maintains infrastructure, licensing and vendor relationships

Budget Highlights
   Implementation of Cityworks software to improve creation and management of
   building permits and applications (including e- permitting)
