Collector Sewer Liftstations
Rehabilitation
Department: Water and Sewer Utility
    Project: Collector Sewer Liftstations Rehabilitation
Project Description
Replacement of obsolete liftstation equipment, which will minimize downtime and
increase resiliency and capacity. This project includes upgrades to the Charlottetown
collector sewer liftstations, replacement of pumps, electronic controllers and monitoring
equipment.
