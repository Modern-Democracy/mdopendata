Public Works Large Fleet Replacement
Department: Public Works
    Project: Public Works Large Fleet Replacement

Project Description
3 plow trucks were ordered last year and are expected to be delivered in the 2025-26
fiscal year at a total cost of $1.25 million. Large Fleet Replacement for Public Works also
includes the purchase of 3 sidewalk machines and a new aerial chipper for the
Environment and Sustainability Department for a total $1.21 million.
