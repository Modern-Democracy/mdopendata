Economic, Tourism and Cultural
Development
Department Summary
The Economic, Tourism and Cultural Development Department drives inclusive
economic growth, helps shape the visitor experience, enhances residents’ quality of life
and civic pride and contributes to building community capital. It works with partners to
advance economic prosperity and manages public events, public art, cultural
development, tourism, civic celebrations and sport tourism events, among others.

Programs and Services Provided
   Increased investment
   Business and tourism attraction and development
   Arts and cultural development
   Event hosting support and delivery of civic celebrations
   Newcomer engagement
   Visitor Information Centre Operation

Budget Highlights
   Further development of an economic development growth strategy and
   reconciliation roadmap
   Enhanced funding for international business development and attraction initiatives
   Increased investment in civic celebrations
   Funding of seven signature national/international events, along with continued
   support of annual events such as Jack Frost Winterfest, DiverseCity Multicultural
   Festival, Old Home Week and the Gold Cup Parade, Moonlight Bazaar and the
   Charlottetown Christmas Festival
   Maximizing the economic, community and social impact of the arts with an increase
   in arts and culture grant funding
