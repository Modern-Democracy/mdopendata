       Sources of Funding
       The City receives funding for these services and capital projects from the following sources:
          Property Taxes
          Provincial/Federal Grants
          Water and Sewer Service Fees
          User Fees

       A Steady Approach to Taxation
       As part of updating the Financial Plan, the City considers what funds are required to maintain or
       enhance these services. This may result in increases to property taxes or utility rates. The City
       plans for stable, ongoing tax rate increases to fund inflation, ensure sustainable services, and
       plan for current and future community needs. This approach provides citizens and businesses
       the ability to anticipate future expenditures.
