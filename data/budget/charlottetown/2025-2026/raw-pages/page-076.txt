Charlottetown Police Services
Department Summary

Charlottetown Police Services (CPS) is the municipal police force responsible for public
safety, law enforcement, and crime prevention in the capital city. As the largest urban
centre in the province, Charlottetown faces unique policing challenges due to its role as
a government, tourism, and commercial hub. The city experiences significant population
fluctuations due to seasonal tourism, post-secondary institutions, and large-scale
events, placing added demands on police resources.

CPS operates with growing challenges and expectations, providing a range of services,
including uniformed patrol, criminal investigations, street crime suppression, tactical
intelligence, and community outreach. The service also supports policing partners
through initiatives like the E-Watch public safety camera system and digital forensics.

With increasing calls for service, rapid population growth, and evolving crime trends
driven by technology, CPS is focusing on strategic growth to ensure public safety.
While their services are specific to the City of Charlottetown, they also work in
collaboration with all other island police agencies.
