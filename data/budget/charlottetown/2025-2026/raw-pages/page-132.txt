Construction of New Sidewalks
 Department: Public Works
     Project: Construction of New Sidewalks

Project Description
New sidewalk construction in the 2025/26 fiscal year includes new infill sidewalk and
paved paths in developed areas. Three sections awarded in 2024 are expected to be
completed in the following areas: 375m on Angus Dr., 80m on Birch Hill Dr., 450m on
Montgomery Dr.

2025 plans also include 275m on Eleana Crt., 175m on Elizabeth St., 400m on Parkside Dr.
as well as the design of 560m on Westcomb Cres. and 750m on Stockman Dr.
