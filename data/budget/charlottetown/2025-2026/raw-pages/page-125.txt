Sherwood Recreation Hall Upgrades
Department: Public Works
    Project: Sherwood Recreation Hall Upgrades

Project Description
The Sherwood Recreation Hall is an ageing building that needs to be assessed for
future services. Funding will be used to generate reports that consider options including
renovations, reconstruction and alignment with adjacent services. This work may
progress to some preparatory work for an approved future vision for the Hall.
