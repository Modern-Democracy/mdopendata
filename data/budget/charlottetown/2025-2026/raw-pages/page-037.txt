Environment and Sustainability
Department Summary
The Environment and Sustainability Department collaborates with internal staff to
manage the City’s natural assets, reduce carbon emissions, and ensure that City
operations are environmentally, socially, and economically sustainable. Additionally, the
department partners with external groups and individuals to enhance Charlottetown’s
natural areas, ecosystem services, sustainable transportation, energy efficiency, food
security, and climate resilience. It oversees programs such as energy reduction
initiatives, climate action planning, management of natural areas and urban forests,
water conservation and flood prevention, food and waste programs, and supports
public transit and active transportation.

Programs and Services Provided
   Energy reduction programs
   Climate action planning
   Natural areas and urban forest management
   Water conservation and flood prevention programs
   Food and waste programs
   Public transit and active transportation

Budget Highlights
   Public transit service delivery in Charlottetown, including a focus on aging fleet
   replacement
   Active Transportation Plan to be launched in spring/summer 2025, projects to
   support increases in cycling, walking and other forms of AT
   Climate Action Plan to be launched in spring 2025, energy efficiency projects,
   community engagement activities, and a flood rebate program
   Natural asset management, street, park and greenspace planting, stewardship and
   restoration
   Community gardening and food security projects
