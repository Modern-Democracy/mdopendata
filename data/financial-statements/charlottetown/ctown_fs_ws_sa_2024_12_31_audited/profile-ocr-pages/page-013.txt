3. Funding Policy
For fiscal years ending December 31, 2024 and 2023, the employer contribution is 22.79% of employees’
salaries. The plan is to be 100% funded and rates are adjusted accordingly. Employees are required to
contribute 3.9% of earnings, exclusive of overtime.
