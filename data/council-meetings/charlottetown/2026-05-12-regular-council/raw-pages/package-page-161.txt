7) New Business
 Under new business, a request was brought forward to explore the installation of
 surveillance cameras along the Norwood Road extension between Norwood Road
 and McCrae Drive, in response to ongoing illegal dumping in the area. It was
 noted that the issue is longstanding but has become increasingly concerning,
 particularly given recent investments to improve the space for residents.
 Members discussed the impact of repeated dumping on drainage and overall site
 conditions, as well as the recurring need for cleanup by Public Works. The
 Interim Police Chief agreed to review the feasibility of installing cameras and
 indicated that footage could be used to identify offenders and support
 enforcement actions. It was also suggested that signage indicating the presence
 of cameras could act as a deterrent. Members acknowledged that illegal dumping
 is a broader, ongoing issue across multiple areas, particularly during the spring
 season. There was no further new business
