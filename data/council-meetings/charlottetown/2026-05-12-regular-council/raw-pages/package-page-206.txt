City of Charlottetown, PEI
 A Bylaw to Amend the City of Charlottetown Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle Bylaw
 Bylaw # 2018-10-A

BE IT ENACTED by the Council of the City of Charlottetown as follows:

PART I - INTERPRETATION AND APPLICATION

1. Title
 1.1. This bylaw shall be known and cited as the "Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle
 Amendment Bylaw."

2. Purpose
 The purpose of this bylaw is to amend the City of Charlottetown Horsedrawn and
 Rickshaw Vehicle Bylaw to reference Power-Assisted Bicycles as well as the Municipal
 Government Act of PEI and the City of Charlottetown Enforcement and Summary
 Proceedings Bylaw, update Schedule 'A' for clarity, correct formatting, typographical
 and minor text corrections to ensure overall consistency throughout the Bylaw.

3. Authority
 3.1. Pursuant to Part 5, Section 134 (1) - Revision of Bylaws of the Municipal Government Act
 R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. M-12.

4. Definitions
 4.1. In this bylaw, any word and term that is defined in the Municipal Government Act, the
 Highway Traffic Act, the Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle Bylaw or the Enforcement
 and Summary Proceedings Bylaw has the same meaning as in that Act or bylaw.

5. The City of Charlottetown Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle Bylaw is hereby amended by the
 following:
 5.1. Delete "Section A: Horsedrawn Vehicles" and replace with Title, Authority, and Purpose
 Headings:
 1. Title

 1.1 This Bylaw may be cited as the Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle Bylaw.

 2. Authority

 2.1 This Bylaw is enacted pursuant to the Municipal Government Act, R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. M-
 12.1, and applicable provincial legislation including the Highway Traffic Act.
