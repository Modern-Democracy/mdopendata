SECTION A: HORSEDRAWN VEHICLES

1. Title

 1.1 This Bylaw may be cited as the Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle Bylaw.

2. Authority

 2.1 This Bylaw is enacted pursuant to the Municipal Government Act, R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap.
 M-12.1, Enforcement and Summary Proceedings Bylaw and applicable provincial
 legislation including the Highway Traffic Act.

3. Purpose

 3.1 The purpose of this Bylaw is to regulate the operation of horse-drawn vehicles,
 pedicabs, and rickshaws within the City of Charlottetown in order to:

 a) ensure public safety;
 b) protect animal welfare;
 c) regulate commercial passenger conveyance; and
 d) maintain public order and cleanliness

4. Definitions

 4.1 In this Bylaw: (add any other necessary def.)

 a) 'Act' means the Municipal Government Act.
 b) 'City' means the City of Charlottetown.
 c) 'City Council' means the Mayor and other Members of the Council of the City.
 d) 'Operator' means anyone who operates a business involving the conveyance of
 passengers in Horsedrawn or Rickshaw Vehicles other than the driver of such a
 Vehicle;
