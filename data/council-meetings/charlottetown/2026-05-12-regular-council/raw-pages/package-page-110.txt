Discussion focused on the evolving financial model for transit services. Under the new
 framework, the municipality will retain farebox revenue, which represents a shift from the
 previous arrangement where the service provider collected hourly payments and fare revenue.
 As a result, future hourly service rates are expected to increase to offset this change and
 maintain overall compensation levels. Mayor Brown reviewed the broader budget implications,
 noting that while the total cost envelope remains similar, the structure of payments has
 changed.

 Questions were also raised regarding transparency and public access to the finalized
 agreement. Jessika Corkum-Gorrill indicated that this would be subject to legal considerations
 on whether a contract of this nature can be shared. CAO MacConnell shared the importance of
 openness with the public.

 Following discussion, the Committee approved the request to extend the bridge rate for an
 additional one-month period, with the understanding that staff will provide further updates
 should additional time be required to finalize the agreement. The motion was moved by Mayor
 Brown, seconded by Councillor Beck, and carried.

 d. Climate Caucus - ES 26-09

 The Committee received an update from Kristen Gore regarding the City's participation in
 Climate Caucus activities, with a recent presentation to the group on the City's Climate Action
 Tracker. Climate Caucus is a national network that supports municipal elected officials in
 advancing climate action through collaboration, knowledge-sharing, and policy development.
 The purpose of the update was to inform the Committee of ongoing opportunities and the value
 of participation in the network.

 Kristen Gore outlined that involvement in Climate Caucus provides access to best practices
 from municipalities across Canada, as well as training, workshops, and policy resources that
 support local climate initiatives. Participation allows the City to remain informed on emerging
 trends in climate governance, funding opportunities, and innovative approaches to reducing
 greenhouse gas emissions and improving resilience. The network also facilitates connections
 between elected officials across Atlantic Canada.

 Councillors acknowledged that participation in Climate Caucus supports informed decision-
 making and enhances the City's ability to adopt effective and evidence-based strategies.
