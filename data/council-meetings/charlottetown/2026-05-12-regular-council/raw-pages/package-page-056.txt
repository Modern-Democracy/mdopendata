MOTION LOST

 Date: May 12, 2026

Moved by Deputy Mayor Alanna Jankov

Seconded by Councillor Julie McCabe

RESOLVED:

That Council approve the request to proceed to public consultation
concerning a request to rezone the subject property identified as 307
Patterson Drive (PID# 676585) from the Low Density Residential (R-2) Zone
to the Business Office Commercial (C-1) Zone in order to harmonize the
existing zoning for two abutting parcels of land registered under the same
PID number (311 and 307 Patterson Drive) containing an existing 1-storey
commercial building (convenience store) currently bisected by an existing
property boundary.
