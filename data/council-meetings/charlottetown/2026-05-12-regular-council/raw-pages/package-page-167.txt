 1.19 "Solicit" means to communicate, in person, using the spoken, written or

 or another thing of value, regardless of whether consideration is offered or
 provided in return.

 reproduction or amplification of music, speech or other sounds, when used for
 communications to, or otherwise addressing or entertaining, whether the same
 is mounted upon a vehicle or upon a building or other structure or upon the
 ground.
