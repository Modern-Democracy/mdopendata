The report further recommends extending the existing median on University Avenue to restrict
left-turn movements from the site and notes that no sightline concerns are anticipated. Based on
this analysis, it is recommended that Planning Board support the proposed drive-thru and queuing
plan and recommend acceptance of the application in support of a future Building and
Development Permit.

Discussion:

It was asked for clarification regarding the methodology used in the Traffic Impact Statement,
noting concern that comparative data had been drawn from larger centres such as Moncton and
Fredericton, rather than solely from Charlottetown. It was questioned whether this approach was
standard practice and how local context was incorporated into the analysis.

It was explained that traffic studies typically rely on a combination of local data and comparable
site analysis. It was stated that primary traffic data is obtained from the local municipality,
including intersection counts and road network conditions, and that the study included a localized
assessment of nearby intersections and potential impacts on the surrounding road system. It was
further explained that comparable sites in other jurisdictions are used to supplement the analysis,
particularly where they exhibit similar traffic patterns and volumes, and that the study represents
a hybrid approach combining both local data and external comparables.

It was noted that while there was no objection to the proposed drive-thru, there remained some
concern regarding the methodology used in the analysis. It was further stated that comparable
access arrangements exist within the City, including right-in/right-out configurations serving
commercial developments along University Avenue, and that the proposed access would serve
not only the drive-thru but also the broader commercial site.

It was stated that the consultant responsible for the study has experience within the region and
that the analysis was considered to be comprehensive. It was expressed that support could be
given to the proposal on that basis.
