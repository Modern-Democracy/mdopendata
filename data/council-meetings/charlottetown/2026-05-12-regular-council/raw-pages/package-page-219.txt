Administration

21. 25. The City Council of the City of Charlottetown shall:

 (a) Limit the number of operators to two (2) per year and shall be determined on or
 before the 1st day of April for the succeeding year;

 (b) Limit for a given period the number of Horsedrawn Vehicles to seven (7) to operate
 in the City in any given year;

 (c) Set the streets, routes, circuits or limits within which Horsedrawn Vehicles may
 operate;

 (d) Establish Stations and prescribe how to park there;

 (e) Set the days, hours and periods of the year during which Horsedrawn Vehicles may
 operate;

 (f) Require that the carriage or the horse be equipped with certain accessories designed
 to ensure the safety of passengers;

 (g) Provide a form of identification for the horses harnessed to the Vehicles;

 (h) Designate the department in charge of the application of this Bylaw;

 (i) Prescribe that the applicant for a Horsedrawn Vehicle permit pass successfully a
 questionnaire designed to demonstrate that:
 (i) he is they are able to harness and unharness a horse;
 (ii) he is they are able to drive a Horsedrawn Vehicle;
 (iii) he is they are qualified to give information of a tourist nature to passengers
 and he is they are conversant with appropriate Bylaw regulations more
 specifically dealing with traffic.

22. 26. Whoever shall contravene this Bylaw shall be liable to a fine of not less than $100 and not
 more than $500, with or without costs, or 90 days in jail.

 The provisions of the City's Enforcement and Summary Proceedings Bylaw #2024-
 ESP-01, or any successor bylaw, apply to the enforcement of this Bylaw.
