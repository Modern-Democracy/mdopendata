Report Title:
Nuisance Bylaw Amendments

Report Purpose:
To present the proposed amendments to include prohibited feeding of Wild Birds and Animals
to the Nuisance Bylaw for the Committee's review and to seek feedback, including any additional amendments the
Committee may wish to recommend.

Staff Recommendation:
Option #1: That the Protective & Emergency Services Committee recommends that Council adopt proposed
amendments to the Nuisance Bylaw.

Option #2: That the Protective & Emergency Services Committee recommends that Council consider adopting a
separate Wildlife Feeding Bylaw.

Report Details:

Research of various municipalities of similar size in regards to feeding of Wildlife Animals within city limits.

Option 1: Amend current Nuisance Bylaw to include definitions section.
Option 2: New separate Bylaw to include definitions and section.

Current Option 1: Add definitions to Current Nuisance Bylaw:

Definitions
 a. "Public Place" means streets, public parks and trails, property owned by the Town, and
 b. recreation facilities funded or partially funded by the Town;
 c. "Wild Animal" means any animal which is not domesticated.
 d. "Songbird" means any bird belonging to the oscine division in ornithology; but does not
 e. include crows, pigeons or seagulls.
 f. "Hummingbird" means any bird of the family Trochilidae in ornithology.
