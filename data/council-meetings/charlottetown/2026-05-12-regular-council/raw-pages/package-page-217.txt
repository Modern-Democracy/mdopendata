HORSEDRAWN AND RICKSHAW VEHICLE BYLAW

Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle Bylaw Proposed Amendments

Driving the Vehicle

9.13. The driver of the Horsedrawn Vehicle shall:

 (a) Have with him his them their driver's permit and the liability insurance certificate
 and produce such documents at the request of a Police Officer of the City of
 Charlottetown;

 (b) Be cleanly and properly dressed;

 (c) Not operate the vehicle when under the influence of alcohol or drugs;

 (d) Not allowed amplified music to emanate from the vehicle between the hours of 7:00
 p.m. and 7:00 a.m.;

 (e) Nothing in subsection 9 13 (d) relieves a person from complying with the
 requirements of the Nuisance Bylaw.

10. 14. Any Police Officer may order the driver of a Horsedrawn Vehicle who does not meet the
 requirements above mentioned to withdraw from service.

11. 15. The Vehicle and the horse shall be kept in the conditions set for the obtention of the relevant
 permits and the insurance policy required for the obtention of the permit shall be kept in
 force for as long as the Vehicle is used commercially for the conveyance of passengers.

12. 16. Any Police Officer may order the driver of a Horsedrawn Vehicle to withdraw that Vehicle
 from service until repaired as ordered and allowed to be put in service again by that Police
 Officer.

13. 17. Any horse hitched to a Horsedrawn Vehicle:

 (a) Shall be properly fed and enjoy an adequate rest period each day;

 (b) Shall be harnessed correctly, specifically in such a way as to be able to breathe
 freely;

 (c) Shall have a harness made of leather or similar material free from cracks and from
 any sharp element which could injure it;

 (d) Shall not be harnessed for more than nine consecutive hours; and

 (e) If used in what is classified as a winter month in the Province of Prince Edward
 Island, shall be covered with a blanket from November 1st to April 30th when
 standing at its Station.
