Report Title:
Transit Service Agreement
Report Purpose:
To seek Council support to pay Trius Transit an increased bridge service rate of $89/hr for 2025 service
expansions, until the updated Transit Service Agreement is in place.

Staff Recommendation:
That Council pay Trius Transit $89/hr for 2025 service expansions until the updated Transit Agreement is in place or
until August 1, 2026, whichever comes first.

Report Details:

City staff continue to work with the T3 operator to update the Transit Service Agreement. The goal shared with
Council was to have the agreement updates completed for April 1, 2026. While work and negotiations are
progressing well, there has been a lot of time needed to review this agreement in detail and legal review is still
underway. Due to this, the updated agreement is not finalized at this time.

In July 2025, Council approved an hourly rate for 2025 delivered service expansions of $78/hr until April 1, 2026,
when the new agreement was projected to come into effect. Staff and the operator are aware that finalizing the
contract will take longer and this report is to bring forward a Trius request for an increase to their hourly rate for
2025 service expansions until the new Agreement is in place or until August 1, 2026, whichever comes first. If
approved, the rate would go from $78/hr to $89/hr.

Trius is requesting this rate increase as fare revenue in expanded areas is low (fare revenue is part of their payment
for service) and they identified that an increase in payment is desired due to new Agreement delay. CATCC approve
of the Trius request for this temporary rate increase.

Council or Committee History:
On January 14, 2025, Council instructed staff to initiate transit contract renegotiations (report ES 24-50).

In July 2025, Council endorsed a bridging service rate for 2025 service expansions (report ES 25-22).
