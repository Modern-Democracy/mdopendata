6. Discussion Items

b) Accessible Taxi Update
 Glen reported on the great news that The Department of Social Development and
 Seniors, along with partner reps from the City of Charlottetown, the Towns of
 Cornwall and Stratford, and community organizations- Pat and the Elephant, PEI
 Spinal Cord Injury Association, and ResourceAbilities, have confirmed financial
 commitments and endorsement of a 2-year pilot project - Accessible Taxi for the
 Capital Region. The cost for riders is still being discussed. Guidelines need to be
 developed regarding who is eligible to use this service. Pat and the Elephant will
 maintain and monitor the service and ensure that drivers receive all the necessary
 training. There will be a media release and announcement to the public, during
 Accessibility Week.
 Glen will contact Shelly Cole to confirm announcement details, he commented that
 the Civic Board has devoted a great deal of time to the Accessible Taxi initiative and
 would like to have some acknowledgement for all the work done by the board during
 the event.
