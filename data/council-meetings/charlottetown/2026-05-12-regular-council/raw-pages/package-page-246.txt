The inclusion of a multi-use pathway will improve active transportation infrastructure by providing a safe, separated facility
for pedestrians and cyclists. This will create an important connection between existing networks on Riverside Drive and
Kensington Road, improving overall network continuity and user safety.

Work is planned to begin at the east end of the corridor near the roundabout and progress west toward Kensington Road.
Initial construction activities will focus on underground infrastructure replacement, including stormwater and sanitary
systems. The project will also increase stormwater capacity and improve drainage within J. Frank MacAuley Park.
Construction is expected to continue throughout the construction season, with a planned pause during Old Home Week.

The Request for Tender resulted in three submissions, as outlined below:

 Firm Name Total Submission Price
 Island Coastal Services Ltd. $5,147,333.00 plus applicable taxes
 Birch Hill Construction Ltd. $5,638,923.00 plus applicable taxes
 Landmark Construction $6,059,621.00 plus applicable taxes

In accordance with the City's procurement policy, this project was awarded to Island Coastal Services Ltd.
as they submitted the lowest compliant bid. Based on their experience and past performance on similar
municipal infrastructure projects, staff are confident in their ability to complete the work in accordance with
the required specifications and standards.
