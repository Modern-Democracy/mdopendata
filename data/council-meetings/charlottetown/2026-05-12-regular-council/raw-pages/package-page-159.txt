6) Reports/Discussions:

 a. Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle Bylaw Amendments
 Interim Police Chief Sean Coombs - Presented the report

 The Chair introduced the reports section, noting that three reports were on the
 agenda. It was acknowledged that the Interim Fire Chief is new to the role, and
 members were asked to be patient as he becomes familiar with reporting
 processes.
 The Interim Police Chief presented a report regarding proposed amendments to
 the horse-drawn and rickshaw vehicle bylaw. The purpose of the amendment is
 to include electric-assist pedicabs within the existing definitions. It was explained
 that under the current bylaw, only human-powered pedicabs are permitted, and
 the proposed change would allow for electrically assisted units. Clarification was
 provided that pedicabs are typically three-wheeled, bicycle-style vehicles used to
 transport passengers, traditionally powered by pedaling, with the amendment
 introducing electric assist as an option.
 There were no concerns raised by committee members regarding the proposed
 amendment. A motion was then put forward and seconded to move the bylaw
 amendments to Council for approval, which was carried.
