Report Title:
Canada Public Transit Fund Agreement
Report Purpose:
To seek Council endorsement for staff to enter into a contribution agreement with the Government of Canada for
the Canada Public Transit Fund.

Staff Recommendation:
That Council support the City entering into an agreement with the Government of Canada, Housing Infrastructure
and Communities Canada, for Baseline Funding as part of the Canada Public Transit Fund.

Council acknowledges that:
 - Government of Canada funds cover 40% of total eligible capital expenses and 80% of eligible non-capital
 expenses, up to $285,725 per year for 10 years, for a total federal investment of $2,857,250;
 - Matching funds will be provided through municipal budgets; and
 - Funds will support transit fleet improvements, including technology such as a modernized fare payment
 system, automatic passenger counters, automated stop announcements and an on-demand study.

Report Details:
The City of Charlottetown, in partnership with the Towns of Cornwall and Stratford, have been awarded federal
funding for T3 Transit through the Canada Public Transit Fund. To confirm this funding, an agreement must be
signed, which staff are requesting Council endorsement of before proceeding.

Up to $285,725 of public transit funding is to be provided annually for five years with a reassessment done at that
time to determine the funding allocation for the following five years. Funding could increase (not decrease) for the
following five years if population and transit ridership increase.
