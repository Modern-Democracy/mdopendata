9) Introduction of New Business

Councilor Norman Beck expressed interest in developing a new bylaw specific to the
Chief Administrative Officer (CAO) role, noting that Council has only one employee and
that there is currently a lack of clarity surrounding the position. Councilor Norman Beck
advised that he would like to bring the matter forward for further discussion. Councilor
Norman Beck provided examples of CAO bylaws from other municipalities and
suggested tailoring a similar bylaw for the City of Charlottetown. It was noted that the
current Procedure Bylaw does not outline the specific duties, expectations, or guidelines
related to the CAO position. The proposed bylaw would focus specifically on the CAO
role. Councilor Trevor MacKinnon commented that a CAO Bylaw would help provide
greater clarity. Councilor Norman Beck asked about the process for creating a new
bylaw. It was noted that the first step is to bring the idea forward to Council for
consideration.

Further discussion will take place at the Council meeting tomorrow evening.
