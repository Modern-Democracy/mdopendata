MOTION CARRIED
MOTION LOST

 Date: May 12, 2026

Moved by Deputy Mayor Alanna Jankov

Seconded by Councillor Julie McCabe

RESOLVED:

That Council approve an amendment to the operational hours outlined in
the Pedestrian Mall Agreement as it pertains to the Victoria Row
Merchants Association, to permit patio operations annually during
patio season between the hours of 8:00 a.m. and 1:00 a.m. on a
permanent basis,

And that Mayor and CAO are hereby authorized to execute standard
contracts and agreements to implement this resolution.
