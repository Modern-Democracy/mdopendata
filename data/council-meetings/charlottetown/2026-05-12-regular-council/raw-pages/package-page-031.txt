MOTION:

Moved by Lea MacDonald and seconded by Bobby Kenny that Planning Board
recommend that Council approve the request to proceed to public consultation
concerning a request to rezone the subject property identified as 307 Patterson Drive
(PID# 676585) from the Low Density Residential (R-2) Zone to the Business Office
Commercial (C-1) Zone in order to harmonize the existing zoning for two abutting
parcels of land registered under the same PID number (311 and 307 Patterson Drive)
containing an existing 1-storey commercial building (convenience store) currently
bisected by an existing property boundary.
