The Planning and Heritage Department also reassessed the operating hours under the Pedestrian Mall
Agreement. Since that time, the Planning and Heritage Department has not received any additional
concerns or feedback from operators or nearby residents related to closing times.

Based on the absence of opposition during the outlined consultation process and the observed positive
impact on businesses operating on Victoria Row, the Department considers it reasonable and
appropriate to update the Pedestrian Mall Agreement to reflect permanent operating hours of 8:00 a.m.
to 1:00 a.m., thereby formalizing the extended closing time during the patio season.

Conclusion

The Planning and Heritage Department recommends that the Planning and Heritage Committee
Recommend that Council approve the Victoria Row Pedestrian Mall operating hours to be 8:00 AM -
1:00AM on a permanent basis during patio season and adjust the Pedestrian Mall Bylaw Agreement to
reflect this change.
