 1. 307 Patterson Drive (PID# 676585)
 David Gundrum, Manager of Development Planning

Property Information: Low Density Residential (R-2) Zone
Staff Presenter: David Gundrum, Manager, Planning Development
Applicant: 102461 PEI Inc.
Reference: Planning Board Meeting Package

Request:
This is a request to proceed to public consultation concerning a request to rezone the subject
property from the existing Low Density Residential (R-2) Zone and place it in the Business Office
Commercial (C-1) Zone for the purpose of harmonizing existing zoning across a common site
containing an existing commercial building (convenience store).

Summary:

David Gundrum presented the request to proceed to public consultation regarding the property
at 307 Patterson Drive (PID# 676585) for a proposed rezoning from the Low Density Residential
(R-2) Zone to the Business Office Commercial (C-1) Zone. The intent of the rezoning is to
harmonize existing zoning across two abutting parcels of land under the same PID,
which contain an existing 1-storey commercial building (convenience store) that is currently
bisected by a property boundary. The subject property is an interior residential lot developed with
a portion of the existing commercial building, which continues onto the adjacent property at 311
Patterson Drive. Surrounding lands include residentially zoned properties, multiple-unit residential
development, and nearby City-owned open space containing a community park. The requested
rezoning is intended to ensure consistent zoning permissions across the entire site
and reflects the historical pattern of use for the property. If approved to proceed, public
notification of a future public meeting will be undertaken. Analysis indicates that the proposal has
merit and that sufficient information has been provided to allow consideration through the public
meeting process. Based on this review, it is recommended that Planning Board
support proceeding to public consultation to consider the proposed rezoning.

Discussion:

It was asked whether additional information could be provided regarding the ownership of the
numbered company listed as the applicant, noting that it can be difficult for members
to identify potential conflicts of interest based solely on a numbered company name. It was
explained that while a corporate search had not been completed, the individuals associated with
the application are local residents of Charlottetown, including one individual identified as a
professor at the University of Prince Edward Island, and are understood to be the officers or
directors of the company.
