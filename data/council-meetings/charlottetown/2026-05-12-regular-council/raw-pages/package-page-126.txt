 Unique climate mitigation and adaptation monitoring capabilities
 Kausal Watch is specifically designed for monitoring implementation progress of climate mitigation
 and adaptation actions. One characteristic of such actions is that it involves multiple departments
 and action owners. The platform allows each action owner to update their actions directly onto the
 platform, enabling collaboration, transparency and efficiency.

 Focus and expertise on cities in design and service
 Kausal was founded and is led by ex-city employees who understand well the general complexities of
 city processes and yet are sensitive to differences in local contexts. The platform is designed with
 specifically cities in mind. Also, Kausal provides continuous support that involves people who are
 familiar with city processes, enabling a greater sense of empathy and better support.
