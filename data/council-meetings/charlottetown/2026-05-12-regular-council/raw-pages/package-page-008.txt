 7.2 Public Works - Councillor Mitchell Tweel, Chair
 Councillor Tweel indicated his Committee report was included in the weekend package. He reported
 that Exhibition Drive Street Reconstruction project will start late April and continue into early fall,
 Sherwood Hall Master Plan public session to present design options will be held April 16 and Councillor
 Tweel proposed on holding a roundtable discussion in the near future on the state of City streets.

 7.3 Environment & Sustainability - Councillor Trevor MacKinnon, Chair
 Councillor MacKinnon indicated his Committee report was included in the weekend package. He
 reported the first of the battery-electric mini transit buses will be on the road soon, 58 Flood Protection
 rebates were issued to residents in 2025/26 (avg. $950/rebate), Climate Action microgrant program
 initiated which offers up to $2,500 for community or individual projects, $15,000 from Green
 Communities Canada for trees at River Ridge Park and $25,000 from Trees for Life for restoration work
 in multiple parks.

 Moved by Councillor Trevor MacKinnon
 Seconded by Councillor Norman Beck

 RESOLVED:
 That Council extends the Trius Transit bridge service rate of $78/hour for 2025 service
 expansions, until May 1, 2026,

 And that Mayor and CAO are hereby authorized to execute standard contract and agreements to
 implement this resolution.
 CARRIED 10-0
