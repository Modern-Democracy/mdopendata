 (c) For the purposes of this section, livestock shall be deemed to be
 running at large if found in any place other than the premises of the owner
 of the livestock or operator as defined in subsection (b) and not under the
 control of any person.

 (d) No owner of livestock or operator of areas listed in 3.5 (b) shall permit
 any livestock to run at large in the City.

 (e) The owner or operator of any area zoned for an agricultural purpose or
 listed in section 3.5 (b) shall, at the City's request, seize and confine any
 livestock running at large in the City and shall make reasonable efforts to
 determine the identity of the owner of the livestock and to inform the owner
 that the livestock has been confined.

 (f) The owner or operator of any area zoned for an agricultural purpose or
 listed in section 3.5 (b) from which any livestock was found running at large
 shall be liable for all damages caused by such livestock and for the
 expenses, if any, incurred by the City of Charlottetown.
