c. Fire Operational Report -
 Interim Fire Chief Tim Jenkins - Presented the report
 The Interim Fire Chief presented the monthly operational report and provided
 brief introductory remarks, noting it was an honor to serve in the role and that
 he is currently becoming familiar with ongoing operations. The report covered
 activity for the month of March, before his appointment.
 It was noted that during the month, there were 36 inspections and permit-
 related activities, one fire investigation, and a total of 90 calls for service across
 all districts. The department also participated in community initiatives, including
 a Muscular Dystrophy fundraising event. Four new recruit firefighters have
 completed orientation and are now integrated into scheduling, helping to support
 staffing needs, particularly in preparation for the summer season. The
 department also received recognition for strong performance at a national curling
 event.
 Clarification was provided that relief firefighters are fully qualified personnel who
 fill in for absences such as vacation or medical leave. Discussion also included
 enhancing future reports with more statistics to provide better context for call
 volumes and operational activity.
 The Chief highlighted ongoing training initiatives, including courses in defensive
 driving, medical first response, hazardous materials, ice rescue, incident
 command, and equipment updates. Additional specialized training was conducted
 in pet CPR in collaboration with veterinary professionals, recognizing the
 importance of animal rescue in emergency response.
 An update was also provided on a member currently on leave, noting positive
 progress in recovery. Overall, the department continues to operate effectively,
 with staff maintaining operations and training activities.
