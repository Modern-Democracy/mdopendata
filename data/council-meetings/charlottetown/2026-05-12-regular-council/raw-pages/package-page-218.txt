Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle Bylaw Proposed Amendments

14. 18. Any Police Officer may order the driver of a Horsedrawn Vehicle whose horse does not
 seem to meet the requirements of this Bylaw:

 (a) To withdraw the animal from service immediately;

 (b) To have the animal examined by a veterinarian to the effect that the animal has
 recovered its health.

15. 19. Shall commit a violation and be liable to the penalty provided for in this Bylaw whoever
 refused to comply with an order pursuant to Sections 10 14, 16 12 and 14 18 thereof.

16. 20. It shall be forbidden for the driver of a Horsedrawn Vehicle:

 (a) To whip his their horse;

 (b) To drive his their Vehicle outside of the streets, routes, circuits and limits set out in
 Schedule "A" annexed hereto;

 (c) To park his their Vehicle elsewhere than at a Station as set out in Schedule "B" "A"
 annexed hereto;

 (d) To leave his their Vehicle unattended;

 (e) To pick up passengers elsewhere than at a Station;

 (f) To carry more passengers than there is seating accommodation in the Vehicle.

17. 21. After sunset, the lanterns of the Vehicles shall be lit.

18. 22. The driver shall:

 (a) When at its Station pick up horse droppings, put them in the box of the Vehicle and
 clean up all traces of urine with a disinfectant so as to leave the street free of
 excrements and foul odours;

 (b) Attach the leg of the animal by means of a hobble strap.

 (c) Pick up all droppings whether the Vehicle is moving or not.

19. 23. The driver of a Horsedrawn Vehicle shall return to the City Police Station within one (1)
 hour any object lost in his their Vehicle.
