 RE: Sole Sourcing Justification of Kausal Watch

 To whom it may concern,

 This letter is to confirm that Kausal Watch is a unique product, developed and sold as a service
 exclusively by Kausal Ltd. The following paragraphs elaborate how it is unique and provide a
 justification for sole sourcing of Kausal Watch.

 Unique Vendor with No Lock-in Risks
 Kausal is the only open-source provider for climate action tracking software, ensuring the City has
 long-term flexibility without any vendor lock-in. Unlike proprietary solutions, Kausal allows for
 self-hosting or transitioning to another provider in the future if needed.

 Public Interest: Time Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness
 Selecting Kausal via direct procurement/sole sourcing is in the best interest of the public due to both
 time efficiency and the small scale of the procurement relative to the cost. The City has used the
 platform for internal and stakeholder engagements, so the transition to its use in monitoring
 implementation progress of climate actions will not require as much work as what it would have been
 if a completely new platform is acquired. Given the relatively low contract value, a lengthy and
 resource-intensive procurement process would not be cost-effective for the City or its taxpayers.
