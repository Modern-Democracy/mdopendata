CITY OF CHARLOTTETOWN
 RESOLUTION OF COUNCIL

 Environment &
 Sustainability # 1

MOTION CARRIED
MOTION LOST

 Date: May 12, 2026

Moved by Councillor Trevor MacKinnon

Seconded by Councillor Norman Beck

RESOLVED:

 That Council approves providing the newly formed Charlottetown Area Watershed
 Association with an annual Watershed Grant of $20,000, previously provided as
 $10,000 to each of the two separate watershed groups,

 And that Mayor and CAO are hereby authorized to execute standard contracts
 and agreements to implement this resolution.
