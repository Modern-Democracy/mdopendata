 RESOLVED:
 That the request to amend Appendix "A" the Future Land Use Map of the City of Charlottetown
 from the Downtown Mixed-Use Neighbourhood (DMUN) Zone to the Downtown Main Street
 (DMS) Zone,

 And a request to amend Appendix "G" - Zoning Map of the Charlottetown Zoning and
 Development Bylaw from Downtown Mixed-Use Neighbourhood (DMUN) to Downtown Main
 Street (DMS) for the properties located on King and Dorchester Streets being (PID#s 336974,
 336909, 336917, 336966 and 1172915) be approved;

 And further to approve the following:

 A lot consolidation of PID#'s 336982, 336974, 336909, 336917, 336966 and 1172915 subject to
 a final pinned survey plan.

 - Payment of cash-in-lieu of parking for six (6) parking spaces and;

 Approval of the following Variances:

 - Step back above the 4th and below the 7th floor on King and Dorchester Streets;

 - The Bylaw requires a 3 metre stepback a 2 metre stepback is proposed. Therefore, a 1
 metre variance is required for both sides;

 - Step back above the 7th floor on King and Dorchester Streets. The Bylaw requires a
 minimum 6.0 meter (19.7 feet) stepback. A 2 metre stepback is proposed. Therefore, a
 4 metre variance is required on both sides;

 - Step back above 4th floor interior lot line (east side) facing Bishop's Palace requires a
 minimum 10 metre (32.8 feet) interior yard setback. A 2 metre (6.5 feet) stepback is
 proposed. Therefore, requires an 8 metre variance;

 - Frontage or lot width requirement for bonus height on Queen Street. Proposed lot
 frontage is 13 metres (42.6 feet). The Zoning Bylaw requires 41 metre (135 feet) and
 minimum lot depth of 36 metre (118 feet) for buildings taller than 21.3 metres (69.9
 feet). Therefore, a 28-metre (91.8 feet) variance is required for frontage;

 - Increase to the allowable floor plate from 8,072 square feet (750 square metres) to
 11,797 square feet (1,096 square metres). Therefore, a variance of 3,724.3 square feet
 (346 square metres) is needed.
 CARRIED 10-0
