Report Title:
Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle Bylaw Amendments

Report Purpose:
To present the proposed amendment to include electric assist powered vehicles in the definitions for Pedicabs to
the Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle Bylaw for the Committee's review and to seek feedback, including any
additional amendments the Committee may wish to recommend.

Staff Recommendation:
That the Protective & Emergency Services Committee recommends that Council adopt proposed amendments to
the Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle Bylaw.

Report Details:
Police have received requests from the public to open a Pedicab company under this bylaw using electric- assist
bicycles in their operation. Electric-assist vehicles have become popular in today's society and are infused into the
general population increasingly.
