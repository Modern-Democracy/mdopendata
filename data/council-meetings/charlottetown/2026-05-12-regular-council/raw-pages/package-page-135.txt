6. Discussions/Reports

 a. Report PW-2026-04-10-003 - Public Works Budget Transfer - Ylber
 Gashi - Manager of Public Works.

 Report by Richard MacEwen as Ylber Gashi could not attend. Report was
 presented to Finance Committee to request a transfer of funds to pay costs
 incurred and 2025-2026 year-end balance of accounts. This is to align budget
 with expenditures based on year-to-date information.

 The original document provided asked for transfers in the amount of $80,000. A
 new document was provided prior to the meeting to change the total amount to
 $12,814.77

 Budget transfers are as follows:

 $6,210 from St Patching - Cold Mix to St Patching - in house Asphalt.

 $4,816 from St Patching - Tool to St Patching - in house Asphalt.

 $1,788.77 from Special Initiatives - Misc. to St Patching - in house Asphalt.

 Total amount of budget transfer $12,814.77.

 Committee members asked what the reason was for the change in the request.
 Amy McQuillan and Richard MacEwen confirmed that the belief is that they got up
 to date information and less funds were needed. Committee asked that they get
 confirmation that the change in funds is not going to carry forward to 2026-27
 fiscal year. Amy McQuillan will confirm and follow up for next meeting.

 Motion to approve the budget transfer request was moved by Councillor Bernard
 and seconded by Mayor Brown.
 Motion Passed.

 Action: Budget Transfer will be forwarded Finance for action.
