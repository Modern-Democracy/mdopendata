Report Details:
The Major Variance applications were submitted by applicant Ashish Goel on behalf of 103580 PEI Inc. on March 13,
2026. The three (3) subject properties are located within the Apartment Residential (R-4) Zone and are primarily
surrounded by undeveloped parcels intended for future residential development (see Attachment B). The applicant
is requesting approval to increase maximum allowable density on three (3) separate but inter-related and abutting
sites to accommodate a total of six (6) separate 3-storey, 32-unit apartment buildings with two (2) buildings and a
total of 64-units per each of the three (3) lots (see Attachment C).

Section 18.2 of the Zoning & Development Bylaw requires apartment buildings to have a minimum lot area of 140
square metres (1,507.0 square feet) per apartment unit for corner lots and a minimum lot area of 115 square metres
(1,237.9 square feet) per apartment unit for interior lots. The applicant is proposing a maximum density of 64 units
on three separate but abutting sites whereas a maximum of 44 units, 48 units and 59 units would otherwise be
permitted for the properties identified as 15 Clonhaven Street (Lot 153), 35 Clonhaven Street (Lot 154) and 165
Carraigbrook Avenue (Lot 155), respectively.

On the basis of maximum permitted lot area for 15 Clonhaven Street (Lot 153), the proposed density of 64 units
represents an increase of 45% over the existing limit while for 35 Clonhaven Street (Lot 154), the proposed density of
64 units represents an increase of 33% over the existing limit while lastly, for 165 Carraigbrook (Lot 155), the proposed
density of 64 units represents an increase of 8% over the existing limit, all based on available existing lot area for each
site.

Staff have reviewed the proposal and determined that there would be no risks or land-use impacts associated with
allowing for the increased density on each lot. There would still exist sufficient land base on all lots to meet all setback
requirements and provide for required parking on each property. The proposed density would still generally be
consistent with the intent and purpose of the (R-4) zone which permits multi-unit residential apartment buildings to
be developed. It is also noted that all six (6) proposed buildings across the three (3) sites would each be well within
the maximum building height limit of 15 metres (49.2 feet) otherwise prescribed under Section 18.2 of the Zoning &
Development Bylaw.

The proposed Major Variances can therefore be considered as consistent with the intent of the Zoning & Development
Bylaw on the basis of the relief being requested in the context of the remaining regulations that apply.
