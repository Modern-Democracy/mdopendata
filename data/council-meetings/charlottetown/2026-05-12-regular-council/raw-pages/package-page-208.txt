Horsedrawn and Rickshaw Vehicle Amendment Bylaw (#2018-10-A)

 shall designate a three and four wheeled vehicle, human-powered by pedaling, by a

 The provisions of the City's Enforcement and Summary Proceedings Bylaw #2024-ESP-
