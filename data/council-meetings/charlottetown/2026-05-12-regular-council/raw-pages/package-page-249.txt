City of Charlottetown - Public Works Department

Public Works Operational Report: April 2026
Public Works staff continue to work diligently across all divisions. Crews are currently
focused on repairing potholes, responding to weather events, and cleaning up after the
winter season.

Winter Events and Cleanup

We want to remind elected officials and residents that at this part of the season, the
department is focused on maintaining safe and accessible pedestrian routes throughout
the City. Any plow damage caused throughout the winter can be reported through our
general line at 902-894-5208. Our landscaping crews will be back and addressing claims in
the coming weeks.

Road Conditions and Potholes
Crews have been actively patching since January, weather permitting with available
materials. Asphalt plants open mid-May, at that point crews will begin larger scale
patching and maintenance work, and the capital street resurfacing program will begin for
the season.

The department would like to remind everyone that the fastest and most efficient way to
report a pothole or snow related concern is through the general line at 902-894-5208.
