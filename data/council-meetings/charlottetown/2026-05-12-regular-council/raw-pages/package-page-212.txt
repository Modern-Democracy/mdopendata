New Definition for "Pedicab Vehicle"

SECTION B: RICKSHAW VEHICLES

Interpretation

1. In this Bylaw:

(a) "Operator" shall designate anyone who operates a business involving the conveyance of passengers in
Rickshaw Vehicles other than the driver of such a Vehicle;

(b) "Pedicab Vehicles" shall designate a three and four-wheeled vehicle, human-powered by pedaling, by a
person, either with or without electric assistance, and used commercially for the conveyance of passengers, such
vehicle also referred to as a cycle rickshaw.

Add Definition of "Power Assisted" to read:

Definition:
"Power-Assisted Bicycles" means a vehicle that
i) is equipped with an electric motor or a motor having a piston displacement not
greater than 50 cubic centimeters,
ii) is not capable of achieving a speed greater than 50 kilometers per hour on level
ground when driven by a person weighing 60 kilograms, and
iii) has no more than three wheels in contact with the ground;
