6) Reports/Discussions
 a. Canada Public Transit Fund Agreement - ES 26-10

 Jessika Corkum-Gorrill presented the Canada Public Transit Fund Agreement and the opportunity
 for the City to enter into a long-term funding arrangement with the Government of Canada. She
 explained that the agreement would provide annual funding of $285,725 over a ten-year period,
 for a total of approximately $2.86 million. It was noted that this funding is separate from other
 transit-related funding programs and represents a direct federal commitment. She further
 advised that a review would take place at the five-year mark, at which time the funding formula
 may be adjusted to reflect changes in population or ridership. Importantly, any such review would
 only present the possibility of increased funding, with no risk of reduction should ridership
 decline.

 Mayor Brown asked several questions to better understand how this funding compares to other
 jurisdictions and whether it builds on existing transit funding. Clarification was provided that the
 funding is independent of other programs. A request was also made for additional information on
 federal transit funding levels in the United States, which Jessika Corkum-Gorrill agreed to
 investigate. Committee members expressed general support for securing stable, long-term
 funding for the transit system.

 Following discussion, the Committee agreed to recommend that Council enter into the
 agreement. Moved by Mayor Brown and seconded by Councillor Tweel. Carried.

 b. Kausal Watch (Climate Action Tracker Platform) Procurement - ES 26-11

 The Committee considered a report seeking approval to continue the sole-source procurement
 of the Kausal Watch platform through to 2030, subject to annual budget approval. Kristen Gore
 provided an overview of the Climate Action Tracker, noting that it was launched in December
 2025 as a publicly accessible platform to track and report on the City's progress toward its
 climate action goals. The platform was described as an important tool for improving
 transparency, supporting internal monitoring, and enhancing public engagement. Since its
 launch, it has received positive feedback from users and other municipalities, and has recorded
 more than 3,000 visits, demonstrating a strong level of interest and use.

 specialized functionality and its ability to align directly with the City's climate action framework.
