Report Title:
Request to proceed to public consultation concerning a proposed rezoning of the subject property from the Low
Density Residential (R-2) Zone to the Business Office Commercial (C-1) Zone in order to harmonize the existing zoning
for two parcels of land under the same PID number (311 and 307 Patterson Drive) containing an existing 1-storey
commercial building (convenience store). The application has been made by 102461 PEI Inc. on behalf of the owners.

Report Purpose:
Evaluation of a request for rezoning from the (R-2) Zone to the (C-1) Zone to harmonize existing zoning among
abutting parcels of land under the same PID number containing an existing 1-storey commercial building (convenience
store) that is currently bisected by an existing property boundary. Consideration at this point is for a request to
proceed to public consultation.

Staff Recommendation:
That Planning Board recommend that Council:

Approve the request to proceed to public consultation concerning a request to rezone the subject property identified
as 307 Patterson Drive (PID# 676585) from the Low Density Residential (R-2) Zone to the Business Office Commercial
(C-1) Zone in order to harmonize the existing zoning for two abutting parcels of land registered under the same PID
number (311 and 307 Patterson Drive) containing an existing 1-storey commercial building (convenience store)
currently bisected by an existing property boundary.
