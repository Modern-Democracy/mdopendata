7. Introduction of New Business

Minna Jane Drive Road Condition

 - Mayor Brown raised concerns about an unsafe and deteriorating private road on Minna
 Jane Drive and requested guidance.
 - Staff noted there is no bylaw for private road maintenance and further review
 is required.
 - Mayor Brown highlighted impacts of heavy truck traffic and nearby development.
 - It was confirmed the matter is a formal complaint; staff to determine appropriate
 process.
 - CAO McConnell advised limited contact information has delayed follow-up with property
 owner as they have not responded to requests made.
 - It was clarified roads and services agreements apply to public infrastructure, not private
 roads.
 - Staff confirmed access and fire safety requirements are considered, but ownership limits
 City authority.
 - Committee agreed that staff will review and report back.

Habitat For Humanity Beech Street Project

 - Committee members requested an update on the Habitat for Humanity project. Staff
 responded that a survey is pending before next steps can commence.

Draft Construction Bylaw

 - Councillor McCabe proposed revisiting draft construction bylaw to address cleanup and
 accountability.
 - Staff confirmed previous work exists and that they will review and bring forward.

8. Adjournment of Public Session
 Moved by Mayor Brown and seconded by Councillor McCabe that the meeting be
 adjourned. The meeting was adjourned at 3:56 PM.
