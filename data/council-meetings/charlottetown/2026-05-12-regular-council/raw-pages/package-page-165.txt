BEING A BYLAW OF THE CITY OF CHARLOTTETOWN WITH RESPECT TO
NOISE AND PUBLIC NUISANCE, PURSUANT TO THE PROVISIONS OF SECTION
64 OF THE CHARLOTTETOWN AREA MUNICIPALITIES ACT, R.S.P.E.I., 1988,
CAP. C-4.1.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF CHARLOTTETOWN
AS FOLLOWS:

PART I: DEFINITIONS

1. In this Bylaw:

 1.1 "Aggressive Solicitation" means soliciting in a manner that is likely to cause
 a reasonable person to be concerned for his or her safety or security.

 1.2 "Captive Audience Solicitation" means when a person is solicited in one of
 the captive audience situations. These generally refer to a person has no
 choice but to attend/stand in a particular location.

 1.3 "Chief Administrative Officer" means the Chief Administrative Officer of
 the City or his duly appointed representative;

 1.4 "City" means the Corporation of the City of Charlottetown and includes the
 area within the City where the context requires;

 1.5 "Common Area" means any entrances, halls, corridors, washrooms, parking
 areas, driveways, roads, streets, sidewalks or alleys of any shopping centre,
 shopping mall or other shopping complex;

 1.6 "Council" means the City Council of the City of Charlottetown;
