 2. West Royalty Community Centre Priority 1 Upgrades
Please note that the public Request for Tender for "West Royalty Community Centre Priority 1 Upgrades" has been
awarded to MacLean Construction in the amount of $529,000 (plus all applicable taxes). This work will include building
envelope upgrades, accessibility improvements, as well as various interior upgrades intended to protect life safety and
ensure code compliance. The scope includes replacing exterior doors at the east, west, and south entrances, installing
push-button accessible door operators at the east entrance, and upgrading the first-floor washroom to be barrier-free.
Interior work includes ceiling repairs, including the replacement of the gym ceiling, new acoustic wall panels in the gym,
and lighting upgrades. Exterior improvements include sidewalk, soffit, air barrier, siding, and roof repairs. Infrastructure will
also be added to support a future access control system.

The Request for Tender was posted on February 18, 2026, and closed March 5, 2026, with four submissions received as
outlined below.

 Firm Name Total Submission Price
 Leading Edge $607,013.68 plus applicable taxes
 MacLean Construction $529,000.00 plus applicable taxes
 ALR Builds Ltd $668,469.84 plus applicable taxes
 United Rentals $674,500.00 plus applicable taxes

In accordance with the new procurement policy, this project was awarded to MacLean Construction as they submitted the
lowest bid at $529,000 plus applicable taxes and given their history of successful projects with the City, staff are confident
in their ability to deliver the work in accordance with the required specifications and standards. This decision aligns with
the recommendation of the engineering consultants who prepared the drawings and specifications, as outlined in the
attached letter of recommendation.

 3. Exhibition Drive Reconstruction
Please note that the public Request for Tender for the "Exhibition Drive Reconstruction" project has been awarded to
Island Coastal Services Ltd. in the amount of $5,147,333.00 (plus all applicable taxes). This project involves a full
reconstruction of Exhibition Drive, including renewal of all underground infrastructure, roadway structure, and surface
improvements.

The scope of work includes replacement of water and sanitary sewer mains, upgrades to the stormwater system, and
improvements to roadway subgrade and base materials. Surface works will include new asphalt pavement, curb
installation, construction of a multi-use pathway, landscaping, line painting, and general surface improvements. The
sanitary infrastructure within this corridor serves as a key discharge line to the Wastewater Treatment Plant on Riverside
Drive, and the area also represents a significant stormwater catchment. As such, all underground systems will be renewed
as part of this project.

From an engineering and asset management perspective, completing underground infrastructure renewal concurrently
with roadway reconstruction aligns with recognized corridor renewal best practices. This approach minimizes future
disruptions, avoids repeated excavation, reduces lifecycle costs, and ensures more efficient use of public funds.
