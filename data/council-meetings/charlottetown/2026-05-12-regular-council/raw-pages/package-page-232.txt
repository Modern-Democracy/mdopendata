5. Arts and Culture Awards
 The committee reviewed the City Arts Awards evening, which was held on March 11
 at The Guild. In general, it was agreed that the evening was very successful with
 excellent choices for entertainment and catering. Julie Pellissier-Lush welcomed
 guests, and Diana Delirio and Xclusive Dance Crew performed. The Lieutenant
 Governor, Mayor Brown, and Jen Redmond, MLA, spoke on behalf of Premier Rob
 Lantz; friends and family of the Late Great Award winner, Gordon Cobb, also spoke.
