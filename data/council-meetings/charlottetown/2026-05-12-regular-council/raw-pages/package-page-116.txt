Report Title:
Introduction to the Charlottetown Area Watershed Association
Report Purpose:
Update Council on the formation of the Charlottetown Area Watershed Association, through the joining of the
Ellen's Creek Watershed Group and the Wright's Creek Watershed Environmental Committee.
Staff Recommendation:
That Council approves providing the Charlottetown Area Watershed Association with the combined annual
Watershed Grant of $20,000, previously split as $10,000 to each of the two separate organizations.
Report Details:
The Wright's Creek Watershed Environmental Committee and the Ellen's Creek Watershed Group have Joined
under a rebranded organization titled Charlottetown Area Watershed Association (CAWA). The Province of PEI
approved the change, and CAWA's boundaries now include the areas previously covered by the two independent
groups.

Charlottetown Area Watershed Association is a community-driven, non-profit organization dedicated to protecting
and restoring the health of local watersheds. Through hands-on restoration projects, water quality monitoring, and
public engagement, they work to keep streams, wetlands, and natural areas clean and vibrant for people and
wildlife.

Supporting groups like CAWA provides many benefits to the City and residents (highlighted at the end of this
report). Funding supports staff wages, tree planting, restoration and enhancement activities, public events,
educational opportunities for students, and monitoring the health of Charlottetown's watersheds. The direct
benefits of working in partnership with CAWA include:

 - Trees/shrubs purchased from the provincial nursery at a significant discount (some species 6-10x cheaper
 than trees that City staff have access to)
 - Staff and volunteer support for City lead initiatives
 - In-depth knowledge of the local watersheds and issues
 - Strong relationship with community members
 - Ability to support work on private lands in partnership with landowners
 - Access to a blanket Watercourse, Wetland and Buffer Zone Activity Permit for Watercourse enhancement
 or maintenance in Charlottetown
 - Access to funding by partnering on grant applications where municipalities cannot be the primary applicant
