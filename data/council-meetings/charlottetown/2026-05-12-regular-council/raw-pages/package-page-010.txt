 7.11 New Business

 Moved by Councillor Norman Beck
 Seconded by Councillor Trevor MacKinnon

 RESOLVED:
 WHEREAS Section 40 of the Municipal Government Act of Prince Edward Island requires Council
 to appoint a Municipal Electoral Officer for election proceedings;

 BE IT RESOLVED THAT the City of Charlottetown requests Elections PEI to oversee and conduct
 the Municipal Election scheduled for November 2, 2026, and

 Further that Tim Garrity be appointed as Municipal Electoral Officer and Stephanie Thorpe be
 appointed as Deputy Municipal Electoral Officer for this election.
 CARRIED 10-0
