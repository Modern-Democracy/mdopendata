Summary:

David Gundrum presented a request to proceed to public consultation regarding the rezoning of
unaddressed properties on Towers Road (PID#s 390534 and 1179670) from the Comprehensive
Development Area (CDA) Zone to the Shopping Centre Commercial (C-3) Zone and to the Mixed
Use Corridor (MUC) Zone to facilitate future commercial development on both sites at different
scales. The property identified as PID# 390534 is a vacant interior lot approximately 2.79 hectares
in area with frontage along Towers Road, abutting the Confederation Trail, undeveloped lands,
and partially developed residential lands, and is intended to accommodate larger-scale retail and
commercial development. The property identified as PID# 1179670 is a smaller vacant corner lot
approximately 0.32 hectares in area with frontage along Towers Road and Mount Edward Road,
abutting existing commercial and residential development, and is intended to
accommodate neighbourhood-oriented commercial uses in the form of a commercial plaza. The
requested rezoning across both parcels is intended to support both larger-scale and smaller-scale
commercial development consistent with their context. If approved to proceed, public notification
of a future public meeting will be undertaken. Analysis indicates that the proposal has merit and
that sufficient information has been provided to allow consideration through the public meeting
process. Based on this review, it is recommended that Planning Board support proceeding to
public consultation to consider the proposed rezonings.

Discussion:

It was asked whether a previously approved smaller apartment building, estimated
at approximately 30 units, had been approved on the corner portion of the subject lands. It was
noted that there was some recollection of a prior approval related to the site, though confirmation
was not immediately available. The applicant was then invited to speak to the matter and provide
clarification.

It was stated by the applicant that additional planning had been undertaken since submission of
the proposal and that an overall vision for the site had evolved. It was explained that a
development agreement had previously been approved for the property following extensive
consultation, which included approval for a small-scale commercial plaza on the corner lot. It was
further stated that a subsequent proposal for a three-storey residential building on that parcel
had been considered but did not proceed.

It was explained that, based on recent market analysis and investment in nearby commercial
properties, including upgrades and new commercial tenants at the adjacent shopping centre,
there is a greater demand for additional commercial space rather than residential development
on that portion of the site. It was stated that the intent is to expand the commercial base with a
mix of retail uses, including a potential large-format tenant and smaller commercial units, along
with a restaurant pad.

It was noted that the proposal represents a shift from the original concept, with commercial
development concentrated along the frontage areas and the potential for higher-density
residential development to be considered internally on the site at a later stage. It was
also stated that the proposed development is intended to complement existing commercial uses
and reflects current market conditions.
