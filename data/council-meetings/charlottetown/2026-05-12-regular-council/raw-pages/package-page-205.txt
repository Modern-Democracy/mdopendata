CITY OF CHARLOTTETOWN
 HORSEDRAWN AND RICKSHAW VEHICLE BYLAW
 AMENDMENT BYLAW #2018-10-A

The purpose of this bylaw is to amend the City of Charlottetown Horsedrawn and Rickshaw
Vehicle Bylaw to reference Power-Assisted Bicycles as well as the Municipal Government Act of
PEI and the City of Charlottetown Enforcement and Summary Proceedings Bylaw, update
Schedule 'A' for clarity, correct formatting, typographical and minor text corrections to ensure
overall consistency throughout the Bylaw.
 1st Reading

 RESOLVED: That the bylaw to amend the CITY OF CHARLOTTETOWN HORSEDRAWN AND
 RICKSHAW VEHICLE BYLAW, #2018-10 be read a first time.

 Moved by Councillor ______________________________________________________Julie McCabe

 Seconded by Councillor____________________________________________________Kevin Ramsay
 Date: May 12, 2026

 RESOLVED: That the bylaw now be approved as a City Bylaw and that it be entitled the
 CITY OF CHARLOTTETOWN HORSEDRAWN AND RICKSHAW VEHICLE BYLAW, #2018-10 and
 that it be read a second time at the next public meeting of Council.
