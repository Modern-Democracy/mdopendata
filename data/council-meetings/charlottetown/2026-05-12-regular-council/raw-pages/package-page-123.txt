Report Title:
Kausal Watch (Climate Action Tracker Platform) Procurement

Report Purpose:
To request Council approval for the annual sole source of the Kausal Watch platform until 2030, subject to annual
operating budget approval.
Staff Recommendation:
That Council approves the annual sole source of the Kausal Watch platform until 2030, subject to annual operating
budget approval.
Report Details:
On December 10, 2025, the City of Charlottetown launched the Climate Action Tracker (CAT), a publicly accessible
online platform to support transparent monitoring and reporting on progress toward the City's Climate Action Plan
(CAP). The platform represents a key implementation tool of the Plan, improving accountability, data visualization,
and public engagement around climate action.

The CAT mirrors the structure of the CAP, allowing users to navigate seamlessly through the Plan's themes, goals,
and individual actions. Each action includes updates and links to related KPIs and targets, enabling visualization of
measurable progress.

Since its launch, the CAT has proven to be a successful and effective tool for both internal monitoring and public
communication. Community members, platform users, and other municipalities have provided positive feedback,
noting the platform's clarity, accessibility, and transparency. Platform analytics further demonstrate strong
engagement, with over 3,000 page visits recorded since launch from users within Charlottetown and across North
America.

The Climate Action Tracker is built on the Kausal Watch platform, a specialized, subscription-based software
designed specifically for climate action monitoring and reporting. In April 2025, Council approved the sole source
procurement of Kausal Watch, with funding allocated through the 2025-26 Operating Budget. The platform was
selected due to its unique functionality, ability to align directly with Charlottetown's Climate Action Plan, and its
proven suitability for municipal climate monitoring. Based on staff's review, there are no comparable alternative
platforms currently available that provide the same level of integration, visualization, and usability.
