CITY OF CHARLOTTETOWN
 RESOLUTION OF COUNCIL #_______________

 Environment &
 Sustainability # 2

MOTION CARRIED
MOTION LOST

 Date: May 12, 2026

Moved by Councillor Trevor MacKinnon

Seconded by Councillor Mitchell Tweel

RESOLVED:

 That Council support the City entering into an agreement with the Government of
 Canada, Housing Infrastructure and Communities Canada, for Baseline Funding as
 part of the Canada Public Transit Fund, and

 That Council acknowledges the following:
 - Government of Canada funds cover 40% of total eligible capital expenses and
 80% of eligible non-capital expenses, up to $285,725 per year for 10 years, for a
 total federal investment of $2,857,250;
 - Matching funds will be provided through municipal budgets; and
 - Funds will support transit fleet improvements, including technology such as a
 modernized fare payment system, automatic passenger counters, automated
 stop announcements and an on-demand study;

 And that Mayor and CAO are hereby authorized to execute standard contracts and
 agreements to implement this resolution.
