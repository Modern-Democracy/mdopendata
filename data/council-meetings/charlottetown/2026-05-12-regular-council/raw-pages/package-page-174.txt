 Injuring Trees

3.16 Unless otherwise authorized, any person who cuts down, de-barks, lops or
 otherwise injures any tree, boxes, supports or other appendages to or around
 any such tree of the City is guilty of an offence.

 General

3.17 Any person who commits a nuisance or permits or causes any nuisance to
 be committed is guilty of an offence.

 Solicitation

3.18 (a) A person shall not solicit in an aggressive manner in any public place.

 (b) For the purpose of this section, and without limiting the generality of
 the phrase, a person shall be considered to be soliciting in an
 "aggressive manner" if they:
 (i) obstruct or impede the passage of another person;
 (ii) make continued requests or solicitations after receiving a
 negative response from another person
 (iii) insult, threaten, coerce or intimidate another person;
 (iii) make physical contact with another person; or
 (iv) are intoxicated by alcohol or under the influence of illegal
 drugs; while soliciting.

3.19 Notwithstanding Section 3.18 (a) and (b), no person shall solicit on a
 street, sidewalk or other public place within 5 meters of what is
 considered a captive audience, which includes:
 (i) a person who is using, waiting to use, or departing from an automated
 teller machine;
 (ii) a person who is using or waiting to use a pay telephone or a public
 toilet facility;
 (iii) a person who is waiting at a taxi stand or a public transit stop;
