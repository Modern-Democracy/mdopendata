Historically, the property tax was one of the earliest taxes used by any government and it has become
almost exclusively limited to use by local governments in North America as national, provincial, and state
governments have made increasing use of income, sales, and excise taxes. Newfoundland-Labrador and
Quebec have retained provincial property taxes, as does British Columbia with its provincial rural and
school property taxes. Ontario and Alberta also levy provincial property taxes for schools.
