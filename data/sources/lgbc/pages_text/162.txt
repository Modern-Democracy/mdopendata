9.7     Summary Observations on Human Services
Human services, which are generally provided to people rather than to property, tend to be labour
intensive, hard to measure, personally provided, and sometimes politically sensitive. Nevertheless, the
services are delivered not only by government employees but also through contractual, volunteer, and
other arrangements.
    The list of local government functions and activities examined in Chapters 6 through 9 is not exhaus-
tive but they do account for the vast majority of local government expenditures. They also illustrate the
variety and nature of local government services and the diversity of institutional arrangements used to
provide and produce them.
