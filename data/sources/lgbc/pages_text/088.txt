Functions
The local government powers of the Islands Trust include regional planning, community planning, land
use regulation, subdivision control, and soil deposit and removal regulation for the islands within its
jurisdiction. When it was first established, the governing council included three provincially appointed
and twenty-six locally elected trustees and the Trust had only an advisory and veto power with respect to
regional district plans. In 1976–78, the provincial appointees were eliminated and the Trust was given
direct authority to prepare plans and land use bylaws. In 1990, the Islands Trust Act was amended to
establish the Islands Trust as a local government with planning and regulatory authority similar to that of
a regional district. The Act also provides for the participation of the municipality of Bowen Island, which
was incorporated in 1999, and any other municipalities created in the trust area.
