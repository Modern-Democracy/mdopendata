   Local Government in British Columbia

T    HIS IS A BOOK THAT BELONGS IN THE LIBRARY OF EVERY BRITISH COLUMBIA RESIDENT.
     A complete primer on all levels of local government, from municipalities and regional districts, to school dis-
tricts and First Nations governments, this volume examines how each government works and its role in our society.
     This fourth edition is completely revised and updated to reflect current legislation and local govern-
ment practice while retaining its clear description of the decisions that affect the quality of life for everyone.
     How efficiently does your community manage its water supply, road construction, or fire depart-
ment? Local Government in British Columbia raises some important questions; it is an indispensable tool for
public servants, community groups—anyone interested in how the system works and how it could work better.
