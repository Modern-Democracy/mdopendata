This page drafts a source-specific clarification request about the unfunded or omitted Vehicle Equipment capital project profile in Charlottetown's approved 2026/2027 budget.

# Vehicle Equipment Clarification Request Draft

## Confirmed Context

Charlottetown City Council approved the 2026/2027 operating and capital budgets on March 31, 2026. The published Police capital schedule contains four detail lines:

- Specialized Equipment: $128,000
- Communication Equipment: $93,000
- Investigative Tools & Tech: $259,000
- Community Safety: $14,000

These amounts total $494,000, exactly matching both `Total Police` and `Net Total Police`. The same financial plan includes a `Vehicle Equipment` project profile describing equipment for new and existing police vehicles, but the Police capital schedule has no Vehicle Equipment line or unallocated residual.

## Draft Request

**Subject:** Clarification requested: 2026/2027 Police Vehicle Equipment capital project

Hello,

I am reviewing the City of Charlottetown's approved 2026/2027 Capital Budget and would appreciate clarification about the Police `Vehicle Equipment` project profile.

The Police capital schedule lists Specialized Equipment ($128,000), Communication Equipment ($93,000), Investigative Tools & Tech ($259,000), and Community Safety ($14,000). These four items total $494,000, matching the reported Police total and net total. The document also contains a Vehicle Equipment project profile describing outfitting for new and existing police vehicles, but I cannot identify a corresponding funded schedule line.

Could the City confirm which of the following applies?

1. Vehicle Equipment is included within one of the four listed Police capital lines. If so, which line and amount apply?
2. Vehicle Equipment was not funded in the approved 2026/2027 budget, and the profile remained in the document inadvertently.
3. A funded Vehicle Equipment line or amount was omitted from the published Police schedule.
4. Another treatment applies. If so, please identify the authoritative budget line and amount.

If available, please also provide or identify the approved supporting schedule or Council record that resolves the discrepancy.

Thank you.
